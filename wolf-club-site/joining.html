Joining Wolf Club

So, you seek to enter the Club? You're in the right place! On this page, you'll find everything you need to know about how to create a character suitable for involvement in Wolf Club!

  • WC is a closed group with a member cap of 75.
  • WC will announce openings when the member count drops, though it is currently up to admin discretion when these happen. These openings will be mentioned in advance, so any potential joiners should keep an eye on our journals!
  • We have a minimum age cap of 18 years, simply for the presence of mature themes that often occur in stories and RP.

What can I do while waiting for an opening?

Read the rules!

Seriously, they're there for a reason. Read through WC's rules, bones information, and this guide! Being able to understand and follow the rules of the Club are imperative. People who do not follow the correct joining procedures or submit incomplete applications will be declined entry to the group and be advised to reread the handbook in order to join.

Be mature!

Yeah, we know, duh! But it's a bit more involved, so read up! Your behavior during and prior to applying to WC is taken into account. If you have a history of harassment, hostility, or general immaturity, this will hurt your chances of being allowed to join the group. Showing a disregard for the rules and/or administrative teams, unwillingness to cooperate, or lack of consideration for others' feelings is unacceptable. WC is a very small community and as such, it's imperative that members get along and settle conflicts honestly.

Read about the packs!

Whichever faction(s) you hope to join, make sure to read up on it! Each pack has a unique culture and that should be taken into account when creating a character. For instance, if you wanted to join Telcoyu, where sacrifice is an integral part of their religion and society, it would not make sense to play a wolf openly opposed to such practices. Characters do not always have to agree with the customs of their packs, but neither should the culture they live in be ignored.

Additionally, it's good to be aware from the beginning what the different tasks, the pack's opinion on outsiders, and overall culture is like so that you can grow your character properly.

Don't harass members or staff about openings.

Watch the journals and be patient! Openings are not predictable, and harassment doesn't make an opening come any sooner!

Practice!

Continue to work on your art and writing — the admin team likes to see an active gallery, and even if it's not related to WC, seeing a gallery that shows active artists and writers proves to us that you're capable of joining an active group like WC!

There's an opening! What should I keep in mind when applying?

Follow the instructions

Follow the instructions provided in the Character Creation section of this handbook! Applications that do not meet the standard requirements will be rejected from the group and asked to try again when their app contains the proper information.

Be thorough and concise

Although 200 words is the personality minimum, and 300 words for the history minimum, we strongly recommend writing more than this. Writing a well-rounded character takes more than just a few paragraphs! On the flip side of that, don't be too repetitive or contain too many tangents. You don't need every detail of a character's life written out, but more than just the bare minimum!

Make your character dynamic

In the ARPG world, every character is the main character. Though it is often tempting to minimize your character's weaknesses, in the end this leads to flat storylines and Mary-Sueing. Stay true to character. Remember that even generally positive traits can be negative in certain situations. And likewise, negative traits can manifest in positive ways! Write personalities with a mixture of good and bad traits, and give your character room to grow as they explore the Club!

Don't rely on disabilities or injury to be special

Crippled legs, giant scars, albinism, split personality — these are all fun traits to give a character! But they should not form the core basis of your wolf's persona. Don't rely on purely luck-of-the-draw afflictions to make your character interesting! If the only defining trait for a character is its illness or injury, we strongly recommend adding more depth to your character! Remember — you are always more than your affliction, and your character should be, too!

Similarly, don't rely too heavily on traumatic backstories

Wolves can be interesting even if they come from a happy home that they left willingly and on good terms!

Note that the following things do NOT impact your ability to join:

  • Artistic skill — WC welcomes all skill levels! We're here to help and encourage one another, regardless of experience!
  • Popularity — Whether you have one or one-thousand watchers, it doesn't matter! Your behavior and activity are the only important things.

If you are a returning applicant, rejoin terms depend on how you left — reach out to staff via note if you're unsure which rules apply to your old characters.

If you are an applicant who has been in WC before:

  • If you had been removed from inactivity or left without exit pieces for your characters, you start over completely for all considerations (i.e. character fees, cooldowns, Bones, etc.)
  • If you left of your own accord and provided exit pieces, any returning characters retain their Bones, but all "first character" rules still apply when rejoining (i.e. no mutations, etc.)
  • If you rejoin with a character who purchased a mateship while in the group, the mateship will remain active! (If their mate is still in the group OR if both wolves with proper exit pieces who were mates return.)
  • Bones are the only thing that characters may get after rejoining, if you provided exit pieces! Returning members do not pay any 'inactivity fees' upon rejoining.

I've been accepted! Now what should I do?

Be active!

Everyone's participation matters! You don't have to be a social butterfly or an art machine, but in order for you and your characters to connect to others, it's important to stay involved! Join in on group plots, RP with someone new, check out the posts of your fellow members! You can reach out, too! It seems scary to reach out for the first time, but most of the members are happy to engage with newcomers! Start in your pack, and gradually, you'll be able to branch out!

Look around the Discord Server!

This is a good place to get familiar with the other members, find partners to RP and do art with, or get inspired! Here you can also ask questions about what other people's characters are like, how you can work together in your pack, and so on!

Continue reading about your pack and the rules!

Seriously, you need to be familiar with the rules and guidelines for the Club. Familiarize yourself with the Bones system, the rules for the group and Discord, etc.

Check out our handy dandy Crash Course doc!

The staff team has set up a quick guide on the basics of WC! If you're new and overwhelmed by our lengthy journals, give it a peek for sure!
