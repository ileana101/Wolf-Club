Group Rules

Behavior and Etiquette

Be mindful of the group's expected Code of Conduct. Members must be aware of the Strike System detailed in the next section below. (The Staff's Code of Conduct and similar Strike System are listed on the Staff page.)

Member Code of Conduct

Wolf Club is a community of passionate and creative individuals from various backgrounds who come together to partake in creating wonderful stories through various mediums of art about our silly little creatures that live inside our head. As such, we want to continue to foster the cooperative and friendly nature of our community and protect it, too. This means there are several behaviors and rules which will be expected for all members, including staff, to abide by so that the intrinsic, core value of the group remains the same.

Be Safe & Have Fun

Be Respectful and Polite

We have the blessing of a diverse community in age, experience, and beliefs. As such, it is necessary that all members be conscientious of what they say and how they interact with each other. Our expectation of members is that they be inclusive and civil when talking with others in the Discord server. The following is never tolerated:

  • Bigotry/Hate Speech: WC is a Safe Space for LGBTQ+ individuals and characters; homophobia, transphobia, racism, etc. within WC spaces or towards any members of our community will not be tolerated under any circumstances.
  • Gossiping about or demeaning other members of the group or their works, past or present. This includes characters, plots, designs, and OOC choices. This behavior is also strongly discouraged in both public and private discussions.
  • Public Blacklisting or Callouts in any platform of members, other groups, or uninvolved artists.

In essence, be mindful of the things you say and avoid being aggressively negative or forceful about your opinions. If there is a question about whether a comment could be interpreted negatively in any way, it is best not to say it.

In the event a discussion has made someone feel spoken over, uncomfortable, or unsafe DM a Moderator or file a ticket via Ticket Bot. If such a situation occurs within a pack channel, contacting the relevant Scribe is also a good avenue towards resolution if you wish to not contact a Moderator.

Be Mature

Wolf Club is a small and generally tight-knit community. While we understand the desire to vent to friends, and allow it in short durations, prolonged negative conversations or ones that seem to be purely for shock value are never acceptable in our public spaces.

In addition, characters that people write most often do not correlate with the beliefs and actions of their creators. Therefore, we highly encourage having an open discussion with your role-play partners to ensure both parties' comfort and needs are met. This means:

  • Keep a clear divide between in-character feelings and out-of-character ones.
  • Respect your role-play partner's boundaries. If you're unsure about an idea, always ask!
  • Do not pressure others into roleplaying or collabing with you if they aren't interested.
  • Be open about what you expect. Intentional deception about how a character interaction is intended to affect future development is heavily discouraged.
  • People have the right to withdraw from engagement in any collaborative and plot form for any reason.

If a member has been put into a situation where they are made to feel uncomfortable, unsafe, or severely pressured by another member they are encouraged to reach out to a Moderator or Council and file a ticket via the Ticket Bot channels.

Be Kind

Everyone in WC comes from different walks of life and has varying responsibilities and commitments. Therefore, when communicating with each other, be clear and kind with expectations and communicate often.

For members to staff, some questions or issues may take longer to resolve at times. Staff will do their best to respond in a timely manner, but also ask for a little patience. If they make an error in the logs/gallery/etc., sending a polite note to the group or messaging a Moderator to point the issue out is greatly appreciated.

General Group Rules

  • Member accounts must abide by dA's Terms of Service and Etiquette!
  • Be respectful and polite to all group members regardless of age, experience, beliefs, or seniority. Be mature; please note that harassment and disrespectful behavior of any kind will not be tolerated. Keep in-character and out-of-character conflicts separate.
  • Please remember that WC staff are volunteers! Occasionally, questions or issues may take longer to resolve. We ask you to remember that we are human, and sometimes people make mistakes. If we make an error, please be patient and work with us to sort out the issue. If you catch a mistake in our logs/gallery/etc., sending a polite Note/Ticket to the group to point it out would be helpful!
  • Please Note the group admin account WCAdmin or send a Discord Mod Ticket with questions, concerns, and suggestions! This is the easiest and most efficient way to get official answers as well as communicate ideas to the staff team. Do not Note the admins on their private accounts or post the same question multiple times in multiple places.
  • Plot-specific questions should be Notes or Mod Tickets. If it only requires a single admin's approval, we may reach out to you with responses in the DM's.
  • dA Note Specific: DeviantArt no longer allows Group Notes, so you must note the WCAdmin account.
  • If you have a particularly sensitive issue you do not wish to Note/Ticket the group about, you may privately Note/DM a member of the Mod Team or Council staff instead. This is NOT for general questions — this is for member disputes, issues concerning staff members, etc. The members of Council are a good choice to go to for any complaints regarding staff, though if your complaint has to do with them, you may Note/DM another member of staff instead.
  • Please Note WCAdmin or submit a Mod Ticket if you intend to host any large-scale WC group-wide OOC projects, such as PMV's, MAP's, etc. so that the staff can review the materials and ensure that they are appropriate for the group before initiating them.
  • Art theft and godmodding are not permitted. However, by engaging in the WC community, you consent to your character being featured in art by others in the group. It is advisable to ask permission to use someone's character, but generally, wolves can be used in memes and as background characters.
  • WC has a Discord for members of the community. New members are issued a link to the server upon joining. Within the server, please abide by its rules, which are detailed on a specific channel of the server itself, and below. Joining discord is NOT mandatory for group membership after a member's first three months in the group.

Member Strike System

Wolf Club generally follows a 3-strike system for all individuals, including members of staff, before escalating infractions. This means individuals will typically receive three reminders before a warning is issued and three warnings before a suspension or permanent ban is enacted. Please keep in mind that while this system is typical, members of staff evaluating the situation reserve the right to escalate as needed for the safety of the community.

Behavior that goes against WC's Philosophy and Codes of Conducts can be met with the following*:

  • A reminder of the CoC that has been agreed to upon being accepted into the group
  • An official warning from staff
  • Temporary suspension from the group

Possibilities for suspension may include:

  • Removal from certain Discord channels
  • Additional in-character restrictions and/or partial loss/forfeiture of character's bones
  • Permanent ban from the group

*Please note that this list is not exhaustive and that this system may change over time.

Group Submission Guidelines

WC strives to maintain a close-knit and collaborative community. As stated, art theft and godmodding are not permitted. However, by joining the group and engaging in RPs or other story-building methods for your characters, you agree to allow other members to post art and roleplay transcripts including or mentioning your characters to deviantART.

  • All art must be your own. Character application art is the only exception to this rule.
  • Barring some exceptions for application art, we do not allow premade templates, linearts, AI generated artwork, or NFTs for bone-worthy art. Utilizing ChatGPT or other AI writing tools is never permitted.
  • Art used for applications may be collaborated, commissioned, gifted, or made on a base you have permission to upload for personal use. Proper credit for the artwork must be provided in the deviantart description.
  • While designs may be bought, adopted, or created with the help of a doll maker, they must be provided for your explicit use.
  • Application art you did not create yourself may not be "double-dipped" for Bones.
  • If you are unable to put your character on the sheet or extras box (for instance, because you are a traditional artist), you may ask a friend, post a classified ad, or Note WCAdmin/submit a Mod Ticket for help! You will still need to provide an original sketch, design, and palette. Art credits must be listed somewhere in the application.
  • All users should make an effort to submit content to the appropriate folder.
  • Content should be submitted to the Faction folder to which the relevant wolf belongs.
  • Cross-pack or Loner x packmate RPs/Collaborations can be submitted to either pack's relevant folder, or the Loner folder, but we recommend the user who uploaded the piece submit to their character's relevant folder.
  • Non-members may not submit to the group, but current group members may submit fan-art or gift-art pieces, with permission, to the group's Commissions, Requests, Gifts, and Art-Trades subfolder (located in "Not For Bones" of the group's gallery.)
  • Tracing or otherwise infringing copyrighted content is NOT permitted! Referencing stock photography and using downloadable brush sets is allowed, but must adhere to the rules of the content's provider. "Google Images" is not an appropriate credit! Pexels.com is a great resource for royalty-free stock photography.
  • You can create art of your intended WC character as desired before they are accepted into the group. Please note that nothing you submit/publish to DeviantArt before the character is accepted into the group will be eligible for bones, nor will it be considered canon.
  • Content where one or more party(s) has left the group may still be uploaded on the following conditions. WC staff will not make a blanket statement of approval for all shared work between current and ex-members; it is up to the current members to seek consent and approval from the prior members.
  • Once a collaborative piece has gotten consent to be submitted, then it will count for the normal amount granted between regular members toward the current player.
  • If we find out a player uploaded a piece against their collaborator's wishes, the piece may be removed from the gallery.

Mature Content Guidelines

  • WC is a mature RP group, and note that some material may be offensive to readers. The opinions expressed through characters in RPs and artwork do not reflect the views of WC as a group, nor the individual responsible for the material. Reader and viewer discretion is advised.
  • Although WC is a mature group, we do not allow explicit depictions of anything rated 'R' into our galleries.
  • Any content which may be deemed sensitive or triggering must be submitted with appropriate content or trigger warnings (CW/TW) in the deviation description, and at the top of the text (if RP/literature.) In every case, 'CW/TW' must be included in the deviation title as a pre-warning.
  • Submission titles cannot contain curse words or profanities, unless they are properly censored. While we understand that RPs may include this kind of language, we ask that it not be excessive. However, while art and RPs can be marked mature, and thus not seen by underage or more sensitive viewers, we cannot censor titles. To bear these audiences in mind, we ask for clean titles.
  • If content is deemed inappropriate by the staff reviewing the piece for approval into the group's galleries, you may be asked to make revisions.
  • Questions about where to submit pieces or how to word or include appropriate trigger warnings can be raised in the questions channel on Discord, Noting WCAdmin, or submitting a Mod Ticket on Discord!

Discord Rules

  • The Discord Chat is for current members. Please do not send the invitation link for non-members!
  • Do not gossip about or defame other members, characters, plots, or other groups. Good-natured joking and positive discussion is fine, but saying hurtful things or attacking another person, their ideas, or another group will not be tolerated. Treat other members the way you would like to be treated.
  • Personal topics (politics, religion, etc.) should be avoided in general chats and discussed through private messages.
  • Excessive venting and negative-themed messages are highly discouraged. Staff reserves the right to kindly ask members to tone back their venting if it is negatively affecting the mood of the server.
  • If you are unable to privately settle an issue between you and another member, please notify the admins via a Note to WCAdmin or a Mod Ticket on Discord. Messages that do not comply with this rule will be deleted.
  • Wolf Club is a mature group and members are expected to be mindful of their discussions here. We acknowledge that characters and themes may include sensitive topics, and while discussion of these things is allowed, please consider using Discord's spoiler tags to mark any potentially graphic, sensitive, or triggering topics by typing || at the beginning and end of your comment, and including a "TW (trigger warning)" for the spoiler tag content. Examples of topics to be mindful of: abuse, excessive violence/gore, assault, sexual content
  • Excessive swearing is discouraged.
  • Certain topics are not permitted under any circumstances. Including but not limited to: racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia. This list is not exhaustive and WC staff reserve the right to act upon a topic not listed here should we feel it detrimental to our community.
  • Avoid mini-modding. Answering common questions (ex: where can I find XYZ?) is fine, but do not speak on behalf of staff/pack leaders, other members, or attempt to enforce rules upon others. If there is an issue or rule being broken, notify an admin. We are not liable for any questions answered incorrectly in the chat by non-staff members.
  • Please Note WCAdmin on DA or send a Mod Ticket in Discord with any serious questions! The questions-and-answers channel is for light/quick questions or clearing up confusion regarding submission guidelines or general questions about the game. In-depth questions regarding your personal bone logs, bone cashing Notes/Tickets, purchasing of characters, etc. should be communicated via Notes or Tickets.
  • Also, do NOT use the questions-and-answers channel to "ping" the staff team, send them reminders, or use it to have conversations. If we haven't replied to your Note/Ticket within a week (we're typically much faster), you are welcome to follow up with a new Note to WCAdmin or new message in your open Ticket with any concerns.
  • Again, staff is not responsible for any misinformation given in response to a question by another member. Questions asked in the Q&A channel may be requested to be Noted to WCAdmin or submitted as a Mod Ticket.
  • Keep in mind that the members of this group come from all different age ranges (18-40s), races, identities, sexual orientations, fandoms, jobs, economical backgrounds, religions, political parties, etc. Do not assume that everyone in the group is comfortable or agrees with your personal views, opinions, or beliefs. Be mindful of the things you say and avoid being aggressively negative or forceful about your opinions. If you are encouraged to move on from a certain topic by an admin, or if another member expresses their discomfort or requests a change of subject, then please do so. If you do not, you may be temporarily removed from the chat and contacted by a staff member to discuss your conduct and ways to improve.
  • To help keep Wolf Club an open and inclusive community for all our members, we ask that you please avoid excessive referencing of private discussions and continuations of personal conversations that happened outside of the WC Discord server. This includes content from private servers, external voice chats, other ARPG groups, etc.
  • Please do not post purchasable designs, commission information, or other promotional material designed for personal financial gain within the community server.
  • Failure to comply with these rules can result in expulsion from both the WC Discord and the WC group. Wolf Club staff reserve the right to enforce these rules without prior warning.
