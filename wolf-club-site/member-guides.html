Getting Started

Creating Your Profile

Set up your profile with a unique username, avatar, and bio. Share your interests in wolf art, roleplay, or both!

Exploring the Community

Browse our forums, check out the gallery, and introduce yourself in the newcomers section.

Joining a Pack

Visit the Packs section to find a group that matches your interests and roleplay style.

Community Rules

  • Be Respectful: Treat all members with kindness and respect, regardless of skill level or experience.
  • No Plagiarism: Always credit original artists and creators. Stealing artwork is strictly prohibited.
  • Keep It Clean: Wolf Club is a PG-13 community. Keep content appropriate for all ages.
  • No Spam: Avoid excessive posting, advertising, or off-topic content.
  • Report Issues: If you see something concerning, contact a moderator immediately.

Roleplay Tips

Character Development

Create a detailed character with a backstory, personality traits, and unique quirks. This makes roleplay more engaging!

Collaborative Storytelling

Work with other roleplayers to create interesting plots. Don't control others' characters or force outcomes.

Writing Quality

Use proper grammar and provide enough detail for others to respond. Quality over quantity!

Respect Boundaries

Discuss comfort levels with your roleplay partners. Always respect others' boundaries and triggers.
