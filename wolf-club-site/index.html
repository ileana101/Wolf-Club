About Us

We are a semi-realistic wolf based art and roleplay group with a little bit more emphasis on the realistic side. Similar to movies like Balto, Spirit, or The Lion King, our characters can speak to one another, have emotions, facial expressions, etc. But they should still for the most part act, look, and move like real wolves — we're not looking for talking animals in clothes, magic powers, or anthropomorphized behavior. Whether you're an experienced RPer or brand new to the hobby, we welcome writers and artists of every skill level!

Character of the Season

Ramman

Xassa

Ramman is the Cyrus of his pack, but he was never supposed to be. But then again, nor was the former Cyrus, his younger brother Nineveh. Ramman had always opposed Hassan's Silence, and when Xerxes beckons you to uphold his peace, you follow suit. After returning from his years long mission, he discovered that he now was entitled to his leadership role. Ramman has worked dutifully to gain the trust of each of his packmates. While some turned to treachery, the majority of the pack have come to truly appreciate his leadership and active collaboration.
