History

Virtus is a pack still wading through its conception. Unlike others it does not have a grand legacy nor long history but they demand the respect of a pack with one. Their history started with the pain of loss and lessons of life given to its founding leader Beatrice. The wolf was born in a time of turmoil and war; forced to grow up fast and starting on the path of a warrior much younger than normal. Her entire upbringing consisted of preparing to defend herself and her family, a duty that she molded her entire being around.

However when that family splintered so did her sense of self. From that moment on it seemed the world was determined to show its ferocity, teaching the young wolf the harsh lessons of survival in quick succession as she searched for a place in the world to call her own. From these lessons she became harsh, militant, and unable to mesh with the nature of most other packs who had settled to gentler existences after the wars ended. And thus she would join no pack; she would form one. One that stood with and understood that survival didn't often allow for peace. One that was molded by the ferocity of nature.

With this ambition set she traveled across the domain and further still, gathering rejects, orphans, deserters, and any wolf that was willing to devote themselves to the cause so long as they were strong of body or mind. Together the developing group would return to claim their piece of the domain, their own place in the world; from the ashes of the misunderstood, from grief, and from pain Virtus rose and conquered their current territory.

Virtus and Its Members

It is not necessarily hard to become a member of this group, outside of looking for competency Virtus does not judge; they will welcome deserters of other packs, loners, orphans, and those of rough pasts as long as they dedicate themselves to the kindred. Due to this Virtus can be quite a diverse group of wolves from various backgrounds, however it is not a place for those of weak will. The pack is run strictly, akin to a military force and does not have much allowance for those not able to carry their own weight. The wolves of Virtus are expected to strive for achievement and strength in all things, and there isn't much that is deemed more important to them than being a force that bows to nothing.

Being that the pack was formed by a rough heart it currently does not hold much warmth for those that do not fall within the kindred's desired vision. Tactical skills, strength, perseverance, and intelligence are all traits deemed crucial to keep the kindred at its peak; while other skills may be seen as useful, they are "less than" in comparison and not considered with the same regard. All wolves are to be treated with a minimum level of respect, however those with "less than" skills may struggle to attain certain privileges that are often reserved for those that perform in other aspects. Privilege distinctions are often marked by one's task and rank, as well as a visual accessory though these are not required to be worn. Permanent items must be purchased with bones.

Nearly all tasks within this group are intensive, members are expected to be passionate in their task, and complete their work diligently each and every day. Training, even for those of rank, is required and checked in on by the Prime. Ambitious wolves will take these visits all the more serious as only those able to have their merit recognized will be granted higher social status and privileges.

There is some reprieve from daily work in the form of events, ceremonies, and celebrations put on throughout the year to honor certain members and foster the building of kinship throughout the pack. During these times wolves may make use of crushed herbs, silt, and minerals available near their rivers and rock formations to create pastes for decorations and "war paints" along the face and body (must be realistic, as if a paw, or nose was used to make the marks). These marks are often random, except for some that are related to one's task, these marks often change location every time they are applied; the paste is not known for its permanence and a quick wash in the rivers or a roll in the grass will easily wipe them away. These markings are only seen during events or war and are not a casual wear.

Religion

Virtus does not hold any religious belief and doesn't encourage belief either, however members are allowed to practice religion so long as it is harmless and does not distract from the betterment of the pack or one's work. Attempting to spread religion is seen as a punishable offense, nothing should be promoted as more important than Virtus.

Foreign Relations

Outsiders are generally met with suspicion and questioning depending on how they are found by a kindred member. Those found outside of the borders or who have called to be found will likely be met with much less hostility and should be given the chance to say their piece. Outsiders who disrespect the borders and enter the territory without valid cause shall be hunted down and imprisoned for questioning and only deemed fit for release by the Prime. Wolves with interest in joining the pack's ranks, wolves from neighboring packs, and those with diplomatic interests should be allowed an audience with the Prime, but they may be treated with suspicion and caution. Outsiders appearing with offerings may also be accepted with a bit more leniency.

After a proper audience the Prime will decide whether an outsider is allowed to stay in the territory or not. This is not a privilege granted without consideration and scrutiny, though this is less so for diplomatic visits. If a wolf is permitted to stay for a time, but does not intend to join the kindred, the visitor will be assigned an aide that will shadow them at all times and ensure they are not causing trouble, spying, or enacting any plans that could be harmful to the kindred. Their access to certain knowledge and areas of the territory are restricted for the safety and security of the pack. Diplomatically driven wolves from other packs may be given an exemption to this rule depending on the intentions of their visit. If a patrol should come upon any pregnant wolves, mothers, or young wolves seeking sanctuary they shall be given a special admittance. These exceptions alone are more readily welcomed into the kindred's territory at all times.

Should an outsider be suspected of having plans that may harm the kindred, they are to be imprisoned until they are judged by the Prime and other leaders of the pack. More often than not, attempting to take advantage of Virtus will result in a sentence of expulsion, maiming, or death. (with player approval)

Virtus is open but indifferent to alliances unless there is a clear benefit. Diplomatic wolves are allowed to attempt forming relationships with neighboring packs as a way to build influence. Official alliances can not be made until the Prime has agreed to proceed and meet the leaders of their prospective allies. Virtus is open to alliances through marriage or allowing permanent ambassadors, they are wary of verbally-based alliances but not opposed to trades.

Pack Life

Initiation

There are many ways in which Virtus tests wolves wishing to join their ranks. These tests are also a required coming of age trial for those born into the kindred. Only one trial pass is required in order to be seen as a full member, but those looking to catch the eyes of the pack's leaders can take on all three trials. Wolves who pass any test may choose any task regardless of the trial they had chosen.

Rite Of Strength

Best suited for those aspiring to choose a task requiring physical abilities and exertions. This trial is held at the Colosseum where the challenger may choose to spar with any wolf who has volunteered themselves. The stronger the opponent chosen, the more respect you may receive. Whether this trial is passed is judged on the challenger's skills and potential rather than win or lose. Those who are particularly bold can even choose higher ups as their spar partner.

Rite Of Fortitude

Best suited for those looking to prove themselves through their durability and courage. This trial is held at the Great Fall, where the challenger will be given the task of going through the largest waterfall in the territory. The longer one chooses to endure it without buckling, the more favorably you are judged.

Rite Of Mind

Best suited for those wanting to show their intellect and abilities to either formulate, follow, or aid plans. This trial is held in the dense forests of the territory where the challenger is given a tactical mission to complete (similar to capture the flag); they will be paired in small teams that must quickly organize and work together against opposing teams. This is judged based on how well the challenger performed under pressure and or how well they planned, worked with, or led their team.

Rite Of the Champion

Reserved only for those truly intent on proving their worth and becoming of note to the pack. This trial is the only one that is on a strict pass/fail basis. All three trials must be completed with some level of competence. Those who are able to pass this rigorous trial are often favored by the Prime.

Seasonal Scrimmage

A gathering held at the Colosseum that is of interest mainly to warriors but open to everyone; during this event members can showcase their talents, and progress in training. This can be a perfect time to appeal for promotions, and share ideas with those gathered as leading members are required to attend.

This event lasts for a week with each day started in the morning by the Prime who will personally observe every showcase and spar match. For warriors, aspiring, and strong wolves in other tasks this is an opportunity to show off, and gauge their strength and wit against a worthy opponent. These spars are meant to be held in good nature and though minor scrapes and attacks are allowed, purposeful serious injury or spars deemed to be going too far are called off. This event is held in a division and tournament style. The event is closed off with the top two sparers facing off on the last day. The winner of the sparring event shall hold the temporary title of "Spar Master" until the next season.

The Great Chase

The Great Chase is a competitive celebration normally held twice a year and can also come about as a means to celebrate a great victory or event. Any wolf is allowed to participate and aim for the temporary title "Great Chaser" by bringing down the highest quality or best prey.

The festivities start before the sunrise, with every member wanting to join being gathered by the Prime under the light of the early morning moon. Words of praise, and encouragement in the form of a speech will be held at this time as the sun slowly begins to rise; when the sky turns red the Great Chase begins with the full force of Virtus going on a hunt. Members may work as small teams, or individually to take down a difficult prey item. Alternatively those not interested in competitively participating may join the Prime in a moose hunt, which will be downed and shared by the pack upon return. The victor of the Great Chase will receive their temporary title for the season and will be gifted an antler by the Prime. Other members that particularly shined during this event may also receive smaller gifts or praise.

Mates & Pairs

Virtus does not keep watch on personal affairs for the most part. Members are free to date or form connections however they wish so long as no pups are born from it and it does not impede task work. If a pair (or more) wishes to declare mateship with one another and wed, they must do so officially with the Prime's approval. A ceremony can be held if the involved party wishes too. Once done they will be given a personal den in the territory by the Prime or they may choose one themselves.

Wedding approval automatically comes with the permission to produce a litter if the pack is in good health. Once wed wolves must hold this connection seriously, infidelity is punishable by any means including but not limited to the banishment of the offending party if the faithful partner(s) wish; being unfaithful does not promote kinship.

Arranged Mateships

Arranged unions are normal in Virtus, campaigning for the union of two dedicated wolves is something seen as common sense. Often these arrangements are used to foster alliances or strengthen the standing of the pack with hope for strong offspring. The Prime is not exempt from such suggestions and is open to arrangements (temporary or otherwise) with highly respected kindred members or high ranking wolves from other packs in the name of strength and alliance. It is also not unheard of for parents to agree upon engagements early on in their children's lives, these promises are easy to break however and both prospective members must be in agreement for the union to officially take place.

Heirs

Virtus follows the rule of the next in line being a child of the current Prime. Heirship can be given to any child or direct relative of the Prime. If there are no relatives to consider when a Prime retires or dies, they along with leading members will elect one or more candidates who participate in trials that determine the best fit to lead. Children of the Prime are all considered potential heirs and are referred to as Princess or Prince. Occasionally an heir-apparent is chosen and announced openly, they are referred to as Young Highness.

Pups & Youth

Pups are the fuel of the kindred's future and are treated as such. As soon as they are old enough to leave the den they are welcomed and fiercely protected by the pack. The Prime is often the first wolf litters are introduced to before their formal welcome to the kindred, during this visit the parents may request for a name to be given by the Prime to their firstborn or pup of their choosing. Over time pups are introduced to the lands of Virtus and the many tasks that sustain them; once they have reached juvenile-hood they will start to be taught fundamentals of different tasks and shall join a general education class lead by an older member of high standing.

Duels

Duels are Virtus' way of solving personal disputes between wolves. Conflicts such as wanting what another has, personal offenses, or broken promises are examples of things that may be dueled over. There is no need to get the Prime's approval for a duel, only that they or a higher ranking wolf is notified to observe and be a witness to the result of said duel. Preferably disputes are solved before any major wounds are given, but if neither party yields more extreme duels can end in more serious injury if the observing higher-up chooses not to intervene. Certain disputes may be escalated for review at the Prime's discretion.

End Of Life Requiem

When a wolf of Virtus dies their relatives may take leave of work to grieve. With their permission or request the Prime will lead a public vigil where all who wish to may visit the fallen member to leave parting gifts and speak any well wishes or words of remembrance. This will last for the duration of one night before the wolf is buried with any offerings given.

Laws

The laws of Virtus are clear and absolute. Going against them invites harm to the pack and oneself. There are many laws the kindred follow but the most notable are;

  • The Prime has final say over all decisions always. None can override or make decisions without the Prime.
  • Stay in line. Always respect the higher rank & those above you.
  • Pups, pregnant wolves, elders, and the Prime eat first (in that order).
  • The kindred always comes before oneself. Wolves of Virtus need to live for the good of the pack.
  • Those of juvenile age and up must do their utmost to choose and work for a task. Adults who do not are to be judged and discriminated against.
  • Killing another wolf is sanctioned so long as it is for the good of the pack. This must be reported to Virtus' leadership immediately and explained thoroughly.
  • Never leak inside information from or otherwise betray the kindred.
  • Romantic fraternizing outside of the pack is begrudgingly tolerated so long as it is not a mateship, the outsider stays outside of Virtus lands, no pups are created from it, the attachment is temporary, and it doesn't interfere with daily task work.
  • Only those ranked lieutenant or higher may accept an Aide. Majors and higher may accept two Aides.
  • Needless harm of a fellow packmate is strictly forbidden.
  • Do not covet what you can not fight to keep.
  • Never leave the territory alone.
  • Leave nothing to waste, utilize every part of what is hunted to the best of your ability.

Punishments

Listed in terms of severity.

Forced labor

A common punishment used against wolves that have committed minor to mid level offenses against the pack. The convicted is made to work additional hours in whatever task needs the help under the watch of a wolf of rank. Diligence during forced labor is a way to regain the kindred's trust after a crime. The length of this service is determined by the severity of the offense.

Imprisonment

A punishment reserved for more serious offenses within the pack, or for outsiders who have trespassed or attempted a transgression against the kindred. This imprisonment is served within the Colosseum where the prisoner is guarded 24/7; it is common that food is restricted during this time. The length of this service is determined by the severity of the offense.

Mauling

A physical punishment dealt out by the Prime or Exarch. The accused will face an attack with the intent to scar and terrify. This is normally done in private but those of higher offense may be sentenced to suffering the punishment in front of a crowd.

Banishment

A harsh punishment reserved for crimes extreme enough that the pack can no longer tolerate the wolf within their fold, but not so extreme they deserve to die. Once a banishment is issued there is little the accused can do to rescind it. They must leave the territory within a day or face physical expulsion.

Death

The most extreme punishment that is only given to the worst of traitors and outsiders that had planned to cause or caused major harm to the kindred.

Tasks

Every wolf is fully expected to contribute to the pack to the best of their abilities. Choosing a task is required once a wolf reaches juvenile age, but particularly motivated youths are fully encouraged and often begin training sooner. Those who are of age but taskless face much prejudice from others. Earning a rank must be done through the passage of one of the pack's trials.

Warrior (referred to as Warrior, Warmen, or Warmaiden) — collectively the Battalion; led by the Victor

Fighters, Protectors, and the Power of the pack, these wolves have the hefty responsibility of maintaining and helping patrol the pack's borders and ensuring the pack's safety through whatever means necessary. Warriors are held to the highest of standards and generally have higher standing in the pack, as such they are expected to act courageously, and put the kindred before all else. All dangers or strange happenings are reported to them with the expectation that they will respond quickly and effectively; they are the only task normally allowed to kill a lone on sight. During times of war they are on the front lines ready to defend their home, while during times of peace around the den many serve as patrols, guards, trainers, additional hunters, or spar and train amongst themselves.

Runner (referred to as Run or Runner) — collectively the Squadron; led by the Sprinter

Hunters, Markers, and Cartographers for Virtus. These wolves are often renowned for their stamina as they are tasked with long hours out hunting and providing for the pack. They report about and maintain locations of prey activities. On top of these duties they must memorize the territory and upkeep marks when necessary. No one knows the lay of the lands and prey's movements better than they do; they are guides for those that need one and are responsible for mapping and updating the kindred's knowledge of the pack's territory. Anything environmentally amiss within Virtus lands is often observed and reported by them first. They often work closely with Warriors and Agents.

Agent (referred to as Agent, Agemen, or Agemaiden) — collectively the Consul; led by the Tactician

Strategists, Diplomats, and Historians. The wolves of this task are known to be gifted with intellect, good memory, and patience; they are to remember and pass on major happenings within Virtus and may be one of the first to report pack happenings to the Prime. They are one of the most personable tasks as they foster and/or maintain relationships within Virtus and on occasion with neighboring packs. They are to learn all they can about neighboring packs. The information they gather is often used as an advantage for alliance or war, and agents should be able to flip the switch from being a friend to a foe depending on pack relations. They may be sent as sleeper agents in other packs during more dire times. In less than peaceful times they will use their knowledge of their enemies to create plans aimed at weaknesses. During times of peace, outside of their diplomatic tasks, they are relied upon to help with pack efficiency; whether it be devising more effective routes for patrols, assisting with organizing hunting parties, or taking statements of events in the pack.

Mender (referred to as Healer, Mender, or Mend) — collectively the Ward; led by the Core

The Healers, Herbal Cultivators, and Crafters of the kindred. Menders are charged with caring for the well-being of their pack members ranging from injuries of all degree, to simple supplemental care. They are also tasked with cultivating, collecting, and testing different herbs and floral goods for their uses both for healing and for battle. During times of war they are fast acting medics that guide the injured from the field, lay poisoned traps for enemies, and can work well under pressure. They are very skilled with finer details and may also assist in utilizing leftovers of prey items either for healing or other purposes. On an average day to day they spend their time formulating treatments, fostering herbal growth, and working with Runners to find new areas to gather material from.

Aide (referred to as Aides, Aidesmen, or Aidesmaiden) — collectively the Administration; led by the Advisor

Assistants, Messengers, and Stewards within the pack. This task is generally regarded with the least respect, however they have the highest chance for social advancement than other tasks. Aides fill in anywhere that needs assistance, they are wolves either adept at multitasking or following directions well. Wolves of this task have two paths they may follow; being an all-around Aide or a personal one. A talented personal Aide can aspire to be assigned or chosen by a wolf of high standing with which they can also elevate their own standing. Once they are the personal Aide to another their tasks shift to assisting that wolf in any way they can, this can vary from running errands, relaying confidential messages, providing counsel, and even helping raise their pups. Aides are not forever bound and may end their contract with an individual with due cause. During time of war they are messengers and relay updates from battlefields to the pack's leaders.
