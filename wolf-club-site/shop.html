Familiars

Familiars must be approved and purchased before content is created with them. For more info, see the Character Creation page.

Familiar

A companion to your wolf. Items may be purchased for a familiar within reason. All species must be found within Canada, the US, and northern Mexico.

Species can include:

  • Bats
  • Birds
  • Reptiles
  • Small rodents and mustelids
  • Can be up to opossums in size.

Scars and accessories must be approved and purchased for familiars before applying them.

Familiars may be transferred for free between characters owned by the user. If the familiar is transferred to another player, that player must purchase the slot first and rewrite / redraw the application themselves and submit it for approval as per usual.

Familiars can be bought for characters using another of your own character's bones, and can be brought in alongside a new character.

30 🦴

For information relating to mates and litters, see the Mates and Pups page!

Bonded Mates

To become bonded mates, characters must:

  • Provide 3 pieces detailing their budding relationship.
  • Have expressed their feelings for at least 1 IRL month.
  • Must wait 1 IRL month AFTER becoming mates to cash a pregnancy.

Bone Cost:

Same Pack: 6 bones each

Different Packs: 10 bones each

Loners: 13 bones each

Loner and Pack: 8 bones each

Other Creatures: 6 bones each

Ex. A Same-Pack pairing of two wolves pays 12 bones total.
Ex. A Loner pairing of three wolves pays 39 bones total.

Temporary Mates

To become temporary mates, characters must:

Loner and Pack: 9 bones each

Making a Purchase

All shop purchases are handled the same way as cashing bones: Note WCAdmin or submit a Bone Cashing Ticket on Discord.

  • Your Note/Ticket must state what you're purchasing, the bone cost, and your character's current and new bone totals.
  • Purchases that require staff approval (mutations, accessories, familiars, pack changes) should include a reference or description of what you'd like approved before it's applied to your character's sheet.
  • You are responsible for having enough bones banked before requesting a purchase — see the Bones Currency System page for how to earn and cash bones.
