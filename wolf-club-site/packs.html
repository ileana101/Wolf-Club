📍 Loners

Not all wolves choose the pack life. Loners roam the lands independently, forging their own paths and making their own rules. While life without a pack can be challenging, many wolves thrive in their independence.

Some loners form small bands for temporary companionship or mutual protection, while others prefer complete solitude. Loners are welcome in most territories as long as they respect pack boundaries and laws.

Other Creatures

While wolves are the primary inhabitants of the Domain, other creatures roam these lands. Some packs, like Riverfell, even welcome other species into their ranks. From foxes to coyotes, these creatures add diversity to the roleplay landscape.

Disbanded Packs

These packs have passed into history, but their legacies live on in the memories and stories of the Domain. Each pack left its mark on the land and shaped the world we know today.

Kausen

Kausen was a fairly traditional wolf pack. They did not worship any deities and only pledged themselves to the changing of the seasons, which brought weather, warmth, and food. They treated each member as a valuable asset and to be respected; though if a member did not return this respect to the rest of the pack, Kausen was not always forgiving. All for one, one for all.

With their alpha Gregor's increasing age, Kausen made the move to disperse and most of its members moved alongside Gregor and his family to Chandor.

Morrow

A small, peaceful pack with no fixed culture of their own, but a conglomerate of what their wolves believed. They were as passive as possible, believing issues ought to be handled with no violence.

Their undoing was a dreadful disease that their alpha Howl caught, and the pack was forced to disperse.

Vale

At the Northern edge of the Domain dwelt a brethren of warriors as rugged and fearsome as the very pinnacles they claim as their home. Vale was a race of wolves characterized by their unwavering loyalty, resolve and strength. Their harsh existence, set against monumental mountains and vast stretches of untamed highlands, spoke to their cold exterior and vigorous perseverance.

Vale's hunting grounds diminished and their lands suffered in the years following their recovery from a Dark Ages, and their alpha Loki led the pack out of the Domain to safer and better lands.

Dawnguard

Dawnguardians had a deep connection to both the Natural World and the Spiritual World. Alphas were said to be direct descendants of the Gods. Having such a profound connection with the deities that watched over them, these wolves kept many Shrines and even safeguarded sacred animals from other predators.

Dawnguard merged with Heyl when the packs' two alphas fell in love. A curse of sickness and death fell upon them shortly after, which was interpreted as the gods rejecting the union. Alpha Nahi died of the sickness. Most of the packs' members turned to the life of a loner.

Damasca

A clan to be reckoned with, the Damascan were a hawk-eyed, nomadic bunch who roamed the vast steppe in a close-knit tribe. Its members were a bunch of predators who flourished knowing only their pack is able to persevere where others have not. With their newly cemented freedom the Damascan were determined to forge their existence even with dubious means.

A long drought, along with rising temperatures had been forcing food sources to venture further and further beyond Damasca's borders. A nomad in nature, Khasar Vargas was determined to survive the degradation of these lands, and thus the pack made the difficult decision to leave their home territory to follow the hunt.

Blutwald

Blutwald underwent many changes. At its height, it was a vicious dictatorship that valued violence and domination. A coup by a trusted beta turned Blutwald into a haven for those who had previously been abused by the regime. In its final years, famine decimated the packs numbers.

Blutwald was defeated in war by Aryn and its members scattered. Though long gone, the memory of Blutwald's bloody regime continues to haunt the Domain.

Havir

An isolated, extremely religious pack that lived in the far north. Havir had existed for generations, its wolves accustomed to a life of harshness. They were tight-knit and held little room for remorse or weakness, and every action was seen as the will of the Gods. They saw themselves as the only thing standing between the rest of the world and the wrath of their deities — through sacrifice and prayer.

Their reign ended when a meteor struck the Domain, killing their alpha and sending the rest to flee.

Aryn

An old family clan rebuilt by one of its dispersed members, Athena. They were honorable and loyal wolves, seeking to help others and work as a whole. They worshiped a single God and flourished for a time, despite conflicts, the most notable of which lost them their alpha pair and sent the pack into a crumbling era called the Inquisition.

The pack was nearly wiped out then, but rebuilt under the paws of a new alpha, Cassandra. However, after the Hive Wars, a hurricane swept through and displaced many of its members. When a former packmate showed up to lead the remaining wolves to a new home, most followed and Aryn disbanded the Domain.
