Group Activity

Monthly Activity: Members

WC runs a member monthly activity check. All members are required to earn at least one bone per month. It is your job to Note WCAdmin or submit a Bone Cashing Ticket on Discord and turn in your bones so that they are recorded (see the Bones Info journal for more information on how to cash bones).

After one month of no submitted activity, a member will receive a red dot. Once a red dot is distributed, if that member lapses in activity one more time in the span of the next three months (as in, does not cash any bones for any of the three months), they will be removed from the group for inactivity.

Note: If you are only able to do the bare minimum (one bone per month), we ask that you reconsider joining WC. The minimum is considered the bottom line, the rare occasion that you were simply too busy. If you are consistently earning one bone of a month simply to stay in, we recommend withdrawing from the group and rejoining in the future when you have more time.

If we notice a consistent pattern of submitting one month's activity at a time, the admin team may reach out directly to inquire about the feasibility of your membership, and try to find solutions — particularly if you have a number of inactive characters.

Although there is no limit to the number of times you can rejoin WC, know that if you are booted due to inactivity, it will hurt your chances of being reaccepted in the future. Members who withdraw voluntarily (by noting/ticketing the group and completing character retirements) will have a significantly better chance of being accepted again, based on their activity during the previous time they were in the group.

Activity Forgiveness:

If you cannot meet the activity check for a given month due to extenuating circumstances, you may Note WCAdmin/submit a Mod Ticket on Discord at least one week before turnover to ask for activity forgiveness. Extenuating circumstances include, but are not limited to: long illnesses, travel, broken computers, difficult finals, etc. Excused absences are kept confidential.

  • Last-minute requests for hiatuses may be denied by staff on a case-by-case basis.
  • The #hiatus-board discord channel is a place to post casual hiatus mentions to let your fellow members know of your activity, but posting there does not count for an Official Hiatus that will save you from a red dot! If you need to request an Official Hiatus, please send a Note or Ticket!

Seasonal Activity: Characters

All members are expected to keep up activity for all of their characters on a seasonal basis.

When submitting a Bone Cashing Ticket or Note of canon content, staff will make note of and mark a character safe as they cash the bones.

Key Requirements:

  • Every character must have at least one canon piece of content every season (3 IRL months).
  • Memes/MSE's do NOT count towards a seasonal activity check.
  • Pieces that include more than one of a player's characters can be used for both characters' activity checks!

Pieces used for seasonal activity must have been uploaded since the beginning of the season. For the Spring AC, for instance, it would have to be submitted between March 1 and May 31.

Characters that are created over the course of a season will still be expected to produce content before the Seasonal Activity Check ends. If a character is created towards the end of the final month of a Seasonal Activity Check, staff will evaluate on a case-by-case basis whether the character needs to produce content. We will coordinate with the members in question when this arises!

Warning:

  • If you have not submitted bones for a character either by Note or Ticket by turnover to the next season, that character will be force-retired and marked inactive.
  • Any characters who cannot provide proof of activity will be force-retired at the end of the season.
  • Force-retired characters lose all of their accumulated bones, their rank, accessories, etc.

Force Retirement Consequences:

  • Members who have characters forcibly retired for inactivity during activity check sweeps will be barred from bringing back or creating new characters for 1 month. This will be tracked on the staff's bone tracker.
  • If you bring back a force-retired character, you must pay an additional +3 bones fee on top of the price of the character slot you are filling.
  • Force-retired characters that are brought back will start at the lowest IC rank and with 0 bones, effectively starting over.
  • It is up to the member to figure out what happened IC to their character to cause their disappearance and loss of rank — if ideas are needed, feel free to reach out to the admins!

Staff reserves the right to discuss character activity with members who consistently submit last-minute submissions for activity checks. It is strongly encouraged to post more than once per season if you have multiple characters.

You are individually responsible for your own seasonal and character activity checks. Collaborations and roleplays can be used for them, but should not be solely relied upon to meet check deadlines. It's crucial to plan accordingly and to not pressure your partner into completing their parts so you can complete your checks.

For example: If the end of the month/season is only a week out, it might be a good idea to start and finish an individual piece for your character(s) in case your collaborative pieces don't finish in time.

Retirement & Character Death

Character Retirement

To retire a character, we require an "Exit" piece. An Exit can be done in the form of an illustration, RP, or short story, and should depict your character leaving the Club.

Note WCAdmin/submit a Bone Cashing Ticket on Discord after you submit an exit piece to have the change officially updated!

  • Retirement pieces (and death pieces) will earn normal bone earnings.
  • A properly retired character (who has an Exit Piece) may be brought back into the Club at any time the player wishes, for the standard character fee.
  • Your character will go back on the Member List and Bones Log, with the same amount of bones that they had recorded when they were taken off the log (the +2 bones given to new characters joining the group for the first time does NOT apply to returning characters).

Character Death

Important: Characters which are officially retired as "deceased" cannot be revived! If you think you might want to bring the character back to the group, make sure you only have them marked as 'retired' when you submit your retirement, and not 'deceased'!

  • Your character loses all of the bones they accumulated in life during their deaths — these do not transfer to another one of your characters!

Special Cases

If you bring in a character and then find you have no muse for them, and submit no story-relevant bone-worthy art, the character can be retired without an exit piece, and without being considered inactive! Bones spent bringing such characters in will not be refunded, similar to any other retired character!

If you have additional questions, Note WCAdmin or Submit a Mod Ticket on Discord!
