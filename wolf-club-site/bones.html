Bones Currency System

What are Bones?

Bones are our in-game currency and act as a point system.

  • Bones are character-specific — they do not pool into a general player pot. In other words, each character earns their own Bones. You cannot use Bones from one character to make purchases for another character (accessories, rank-ups, etc.)
  • Bones can be pooled for the purchase of new characters and for familiars.
  • Nothing you post before your character is accepted into the group will be eligible for Bones. You are allowed to post art of a character before they are accepted, but it would not be worth Bones if uploaded to your gallery before the application is accepted.
  • You can only earn Bones for your own art and literature. Gift art and commissions are not worth Bones. Collaboration and RP's are worth Bones if conducted with another active member's characters (and are canon).
Canon Content Only: Artwork, literature, and RPs are ONLY worth Bones if they pertain to your character's canon story, past or present, or depict day-to-day scenes. If the submission does not pertain to your character's canon, it is not eligible for Bones, unless they are in response to specific non-canon MSE prompts.

Automatic Bones

Character Joining = 2 🦴

Characters are automatically given 2 bones upon joining the group.

Character of the Season Vote = 1 🦴

Participating in the seasonal CotS vote is worth a bone to the character of your choosing!

Illustrations

2 🦴 Full-Body Illustration 50% or more of body visible
1 🦴 Half-Body Illustration Less than 50% of body visible

Requirements:

  • Illustrations must feature 50% or more of the wolf's body in order to be cashed as Full-body.
  • Artwork must be colored with at least a loose background.
  • Grayscale work is allowed, but it must be extremely polished and possess a full, clearly-defined background (ex: no gradient skies with a handful of clouds).
  • Character silhouettes must be identifiable as your character, and not solid black shapes.
  • Collaborations are permitted, under the same rules, and must include a character from each collaborator.

Available Bonuses: +1 Familiar bonus • +1 Clubian bonus • +1 Plot bonus

Comic Pages / Multi-Illustrations

Illustrations must be a minimum of two panels to be classified as a comic.

How Comics are Calculated:

Comics are processed with each panel acting as its own separate art piece:

  • If a panel features a half-body = 1 bone
  • If a panel features a full-body = 2 bones
  • These do not stack with multiple characters in a single panel.
  • Comics must be colored and with at least a loose background in two or more panels.
  • Copy-pasted panels with only slight variations will not count.
  • All collaborators earn the same number of base Bones for their characters, calculated by the maximum possible Bone earnings per panel.
  • Clubian Bonus is applied per every new character featured. Ex: If you draw 3 different characters you do not own within the entirety of your comic page you would receive a +3 Clubian Bonus.

Available Bonuses: +1 Familiar bonus (per page) • +1 Plot bonus (per page) • Clubian bonus per new character

Roleplay & Literature

Paragraph-Style RP = 1 Bone

  • Minimum of three posts per character
  • Each subsequent set of three posts earns an additional +1 Bone
  • Example: A roleplay where each character posted six times earns 2 Bones.
  • Available bonuses: +1 Familiar bonus; +1 Plot bonus

Literature-Style RP = 1 Bone

  • A 2-character literature-style RP earns +1 Bone per participant per every 1000 words.
  • If there are more than two characters, the requirement for a Bone goes up by 500 words per additional character.
  • Example: 1 Bone per every 1500 words between 3 characters, 1 Bone per every 2000 words between 4 characters, etc.
  • Available bonuses: +1 Familiar bonus; +1 Plot bonus

Solo Literature = 1 Bone

  • Stories must comprise a minimum of 500 words
  • Each set of 500 words after the first earns +1 Bone
  • Example: A lit piece with at least 1000 words earns 2 Bones.
  • Available bonuses: +1 Familiar bonus; +1 Plot bonus

Animations & PMVs

Simple Animation = 1 Bone

Examples: blink, ear twitch, "pixel bounce", and 2-4 frame animations.

Moderate Animation = 4 Bones

Examples: multiple moving parts, lifting a leg, wagging tail — a simple animation with more than 2-4 frames.

Complex Animation = 8+ Bones

Examples: Run cycles, jump cycles, voice overs, fully animated scenes or turnarounds.

PMVs = 8+ Bones

If they are a minute or longer. For every additional 30 seconds, add +1 bone. (PMVs less than a minute = moderate animation)

Note: Animations are the trickiest thing to cash bones for. While we have tried to create basic guidelines to follow, there is room for discussion when cashing animations. PMVs are non-negotiable.

Available Bonuses: +1 Familiar bonus • +1 Dominian bonus • +1 Plot bonus

Monthly Special Events (MSEs)

2-3 🦴
  • MSE memes grant +1 bone in the month they are the "monthly special event" — otherwise, they are worth only their base bone amount.
  • MSE memes with 4 panels or more are worth 2 bones (+1 if completed in the MSE month).
  • It is only eligible for both bones if each panel is distinct — copy -pasted panels with only slight alterations do not count!
  • Individual memes may have special rules — check the descriptions of blank memes for additional instructions!

Bone Bonuses

The following bonuses are allowed under specific types of submissions. You MUST follow the instructions detailed here in order to be considered for the bonus!

+1 Familiar Bonus

Add 1 bone to your total if your character's familiar is featured in the artwork or literature; it must be at least a bust of the familiar and cannot be a silhouette. In literature, the character cannot just be mentioned, but serve as part of the RP and/or lit piece.

+1 Clubian Bonus (per character)

Add 1 or more Bones if you featured one or more active characters that do not belong to you in the artwork; it must be at least a bust of the other character and cannot be a silhouette.

  • Collaborations do not count for this bonus, unless you and the other artist have included characters that do not belong to either/any of you.
  • This cannot be applied to lit pieces.
  • If you include more than one of your own characters, this bonus does not stack for both of your own characters.

+1 Plot Bonus

When a group-wide or packwide plot is in progress, and specifically mentioned by the admin team, you may claim a bonus based on the individual parameters of the plot or event. Members will be informed via event journals when these bonuses are available for art and literature.

NOT Eligible for Bones

  • Heart Charts, Relationship Trackers, and Medallions (Medallion art can be submitted outside of the medallion template for a bone if it adheres to illustration guidelines).
  • OOC Art (AUs, non-canon works, etc.) — Certain non-canon works can be submitted for bones if part of a prompt or MSE specifically listed in the Past Prompts and MSEs document.
  • Gifts and Commissions (Unless part of the official Secret Santa event, which is worth bones).
  • Chibis, standalone pixels, icons, stamps, floating bodies or heads, etc. (Unless they fall under a specifically listed Past Prompt or MSE).

Cashing Bones (Submitting Bones)

To record your bones: Note WCAdmin or submit a Bone Cashing Ticket on Discord.

Note Format Requirements:

Title Format:

For bone cashing: CHARACTER NAME | FACTION NAME | NOTE PURPOSE HERE

For OOC matters: OOC | MEMBER NAME | NOTE PURPOSE HERE

Your Note Must Include:

  • A link to the piece being cashed for bones
  • A +# for the number of bones being claimed
  • In parenthesis, which bonuses are being claimed for a work
  • Your previous bone count AND new count

Important Rules:

  • Failure to use the proper template may result in staff asking you to resubmit.
  • Each Bones Note must only pertain to a single character. Tickets may pertain to multiple characters.
  • Pieces must be submitted and accepted into the group's gallery to count for bones.
  • Bones are recorded based on the month they are cashed in, not the month the art or lit is submitted.
  • Pieces must be cashed for bones within 2 months of their submission date. For example, a piece submitted in July but cashed in August is fine, but cashing it in September will not be accepted!
  • It is YOUR responsibility to turn in and cash Bones. If you do not Note WCAdmin or submit a Bone Cashing Ticket, your character(s) will not receive bones.

Frequently Asked Questions

I forgot to cash bones for a piece I submitted! Can I still get bones for it?

Yes, but only if you remember to do it within a two month span! It may be declined for bones if it exceeds two months old or if old art is being repeatedly used to pass current activity checks.

What is a "Loose Background"?

A loose background must fall within the following parameters:

  • Backgrounds can be extremely realistic to very loose/abstract, but even at a minimum should still pertain to your character's story and represent the actual natural world.
  • Characters cannot be completely "detached" from the background.
  • Backgrounds cannot be just a solid color, plain gradient, texture, or repeating pattern.
  • Backgrounds cannot just be the ground at a character's feet or a single prop with a color/gradient/texture/pattern added in.
  • Backgrounds cannot be more than 25% transparent.
  • Borderline backgrounds will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

How long do I have to wait for a reply to my Note/Ticket? Was it lost?

Some Notes need to be answered by specific moderators or require a group discussion. That takes time! Please wait at least a week before re-sending your Note. If you think it was missed, send a NEW NOTE! Tickets are typically replied to faster as they are more noticeable, but they may also take a few days if things need to be discussed.

Do NOT reply to your own Note sent to the group with edits, or else it will mark the Note as read and replied!
