Mates and Pups

Here you can find any and all information relating to courtship, taking mates, having pups, and bringing in pup characters! The related purchase information can also be found on the Shop page! Further down is a list of all mated pairs and their offspring that have been in Wolf Club.

While your characters may fall in love or accidentally (or not-so-accidentally) have a quick fling, they cannot officially become mates or have pups without cashing the appropriate bones fees!

Both characters need to turn in bones for this! Keep in mind that mates from different packs or a loner/mate pair will still be expected to abide by the rules that your pack has about mating outside of its borders.

Remember that breaking these rules may have IC consequences.

There are 2 options for taking a mate in Wolf Club:

  • A permanent, bonded mate (like a marriage, may or may not have pups)
  • A temporary mate (a fling resulting in pups)

Information on Permanent/Bonded Mates

In order to take a permanent, bonded mate, the characters involved MUST:
  • Have at least 3 pieces together developing their relationship (these pieces must be submitted with the Note/Ticket for cashing these bones).
  • Have expressed feelings in-character for each other for a minimum of 1 season (3 IRL months).
  • Be official mates for 1 real-life month before they can cash a pregnancy.
  • In the case of polyamorous relationships, cost for pregnancy and litters is split evenly among all parties.

What if, for plot reasons, we want our characters to get together as permanent mates because of a pregnancy?

The staff are always willing to discuss plots with members, and all you need to do is Note WolfClubAdmin/submit a Mod Ticket to talk to us about the avenues you wish to take!

What if our characters cannot have pups together and want a surrogate?

Surrogacy pregnancies are split equally among all involved parties.

The surrogate parent only needs to pay for the pregnancy; the pup costs (see below) are paid for by whomever is raising the pups.

Note: When a pair of characters take a bonded mate, they may receive a shoutout journal in the group, proclaiming their love for all to see! You must specify if you want a shoutout journal in your purchase Note/Ticket. The staff may not create one without notification!

Shoutout journals are only available for bonded mates!

Information on Temporary Mates

In order to take a temporary mate (otherwise considered a fling resulting in a pregnancy), there is only one requirement:
  • Have 1 piece depicting the pair agreeing to have a litter together or interacting in some way that indicates a fling (this piece must be submitted with the Note/Ticket for cashing these bones).

Information on Litters and Pups

You MUST pay the pregnancy fee before putting any pups up for free-form adoption in the group! The pregnancy fees are listed on the Shop page. Pregnancies last six IRL weeks.

Should the owner of a parent decide to play a pup from the litter, this pup is considered "free" and the New Character fee will be waived. This reward is meant to offset the costs for the mates/pregnancy/pup cost. This discount can only be claimed once per litter, and may be claimed by all parents involved (such as surrogacy situations where there are three).

Members adopting pups from in-game litters must still pay the New Character fee! Make sure you have enough bones before adopting!

There can never be more than 10 pups up for free-form adoption at any time. Should this amount be reached, new litters must wait until the adoptable pups are reduced to 5.

Names for pups can be reserved when the pregnancy is cashed, but only if the player pays for their pup slot at the same time and specifically indicates the pup's name.

If a predetermined pup player who is not one of the player-parents wants to reserve a name, they must cash their new character cost during the pregnancy to reserve the name. Names can only be reserved when the character is paid for, even if it's a few weeks before they're actually born!

Plots involving miscarriages or litters with stillborn pups can be discussed via Note to WolfClubAdmin/Mod Ticket on Discord, but bear in mind that these fees still apply!

Due to the sensitive nature of these plots, they must be approved by the admin team and handled with care.

Extra Info on Pups

Pups brought into the group must have either 2 NPC parents, or 2 player character parents who have gone through the proper pregnancy process!

Scenarios that result in 1 NPC and 1 PC parent are NOT allowed.

Characters who come into the group as adults may have 1 PC parent and 1 NPC parent, if their conception occurred BEFORE the parent character entered the Domain.

Pups born into the Domain should age up to juveniles approximately 3 real-life months after birth, and similarly, juveniles should age up to young adult after approximately 6 real-life months.

After this, you may age your character at whatever speed you like.

If you are bringing a pup into the Domain, they MUST have a Guardian, and unless their Guardian is nursing, they MUST be at least of weaning age (2 months)!

Pups brought into the Domain should age up to juvenile approximately 1 real-life month (or 2-3 if nursing) after joining.

Guardian Information

  • Guardians can be related (older siblings, aunts, uncles, etc.) OR unrelated
  • Guardians are needed for ALL PUPS. Like parents, Guardians MUST pay a fee in order to care for a pup!
  • Guardians may have up to the max limit of pups (4). These pups must be paid for, using the same fees highlighted in the Shop (6 Bones for one pup, 12 Bones for two pups, etc.)
  • Multiple Guardians may take care of pups, with each wolf required to pay the Guardian Fee. They can then split the pup fee.
Example:

Two guardians with a 3 pup litter: 10 bones for one Guardian, 10 bones for the other Guardian, and 24 bones for the pups. Each Guardian player pays 22 bones.

Mates & Pups Masterlist

This is a record of all the mated pairs that have happened in Wolf Club! We record both current and former pairs, so even if your wolves have split apart for any reason, their relationship will be recorded on this list.

Please note, this list is only for pairs that have paid the bones to become mates and pups (or just mates if they don't want pups right now). So if you have not paid for your characters to become mates yet, we will not list it here. Similarly, if you have paid and still don't see your relationship here, please send us a link to the note of your bones cashing!

If for any reason you see any other type of mistake (wrong names, litters missing, pair in the current but should be in former and vice versa, etc) please send us a note on deviantArt or a ticket in discord!

Key:
  1. L (followed by a number) stands for Litter (1, 2, 3 etc.)
  2. Conception date: The date you turned in bones via note for your character to become pregnant
  3. Birth date: The date when your (real-life) 6 week waiting period is up and your pups can now become active
  4. Player Deadline/Juvenile Milestone: After 3 months (one season) this is the month when your pup should either age up to juvenile, or die if they do not have a player.
  5. Young Adult Milestone: After 6 months (two seasons) from the last milestone, this is when your juvenile should age up to a young adult, you control how fast or slow they age!
