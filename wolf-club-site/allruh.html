History

Before any living being touched the earth of these lands, mysterious entities already dominated the astral skies above them. Not wolf or any other kind of animal, these stardust beings entertained themselves by experimenting with what the skies gave them.

They were life givers, They were life takers — They were Creators.

They constructed countless beings, but none satisfied the Creators' desperate search for perfection. Until one of them gave life to Aeon.

This creature was Their masterpiece, a combination of everything They desired, everything They valued. Just as if she was one of their own, They gave Aeon the ability to create life and tasked this being to generate more like her.

But the Creators had made a fatal mistake in creating Aeon.

Unlike their other beings, they had truly formed this one based on themselves and in their never-ending arrogance assumed that a being as clever as themselves would willingly oblige them.

Unforeseen by the Creators, Aeon broke through the barrier that separated their astral layer from the world of their experiments. Leaving the skies, their perfect creation enraged her life givers.

"You belong to us! We will hunt you and all of your kin, for it is your fate to be beside us!"

But Aeon was far more clever than the Creators, avoiding all of Their attempts to bring her back. Her escape was an omen of new times to come.

Hiding from the skies, deep inside stone hallways underneath massive mountains, sleeping at day when Their power was at its weakest and keeping a close eye on the moon at night, as it was the gateway leading into Their domain.

And so Aeon became the first wolf to walk these lands.

Foreign Relations

Life as a member of Allruh is rough, there's always the possibility that They will arrive to claim the pack and many battles have already been fought in desperate attempts to escape Their grasp.

Despite all these hardships, the pack is open to strangers — as open as they can be, at least. Every loner, referred to as Drifter, needs to be assessed carefully as it is always a possibility that They have gained control over them. Such a danger can not be allowed to reach the pack. Strangers at the border must be turned away or even chased off if they are afflicted by Them.

Pack wolves are treated similarly and foreign packs are always met with a hint of suspicion at first, due to the same fear of these packs being under Their control. It is up to these packs to gain Allruh's trust.

If this has been achieved, the wolves of Allruh become warmhearted hosts that proudly display all their knowledge, though they are always careful to not share too much.

One can never be too careful.

Culture

This is how history has been passed down countless generations of Ruhians. Nowadays, the wolves of Allruh take great pride in being the descendants of the first wolf, yet they still uphold the fearful way of life that Aeon had created.

Allruh wolves, the Aeonkin, follow a nocturnal lifestyle, sleeping at day when They are unable to enter this world, keeping a close eye on the moon gateway during nighttime.

Stone keeps them safe which is why the pack feels most comfortable living deep down underneath a massive mountain, inside the cold rock corridors.

These wolves are gatherers of knowledge, but equally cautious. They are careful not to share too much about their pack at first glance – to avoid gathering Their interest. Visitors are welcome, as long as they are not afflicted by Them and strangers may even join the pack, but there is often a price to be paid…

As mysterious and self-preserving as these wolves are, they suffer greatly under the burden that has been placed upon them.

They fear their Creators, always on the watch for possible invasions coming from the astral skies. But the wolves of Allruh are still around and stubbornly keep on surviving, as after all, Aeon had fulfilled the order placed upon her — the time of the wolves had come. Their perfect creation.

Beliefs & Customs

★ The Creators in the Astral Sky ★

Wolves did not learn to walk this earth randomly, no, they were created by beings up in the astral skies. Aeon was the very first, escaping the prison where They were playing with their experiments and creations, landing on the plains below.

This escape greatly angered the Creators and ever since then, They are watching the wolves below through the moon. One day, the Aeonkin fears, They will be able to traverse this moon-gateway and reach the plains.

And once this day comes, the time of all wolves will be over. The moon is therefore paranoidly watched all times during night, unless heavy cloud covers hide it from the eyes of the Aeonkin. Only the stars are seen favorably, as they are believed to house the spirits of their deceased.

★ Stardust ★

As wolves were created by Them, the Aeonkin believes that every being is bound together by the material They used to form Aeon. A certain kind of dust, Stardust, is part of every living being, no matter if it's a creature or a plant and it does not matter if the host is even aware of its existence. This connection allows Them to influence the living to obey their orders, but it also makes it possible for a being to let their spirit return to the astral sky after death.

From the astral skies they come, and there they will return, but when they return, the spirits will be free and can no longer be taken by Them.

Laws

Allruh can not survive and withstand Their continuous attacks if the pack is not careful. Therefore, certain laws are ingrained in every Aeonkin and need to be followed in order for them all to be safe.

Remember Aeon and all who fell after her

As the Aeonkin are direct descendants of the first wolf, this honor and burden needs to be remembered at all times. The wolves of Allruh are thankful for her decision to escape Them, but they also know about the price this cost all of them. Aeon needs to be remembered, as do all Aeonkin that left the world of the living to take their places among the stars.

Do not allow them to gain influence

Every Aeonkin needs to make sure that They are not able to control any packmate, Drifter or stranger. This comes with a lot of paranoia about stranger's motives that need to be questioned and figured out before these wolves are able to set foot into Allruh's territory. The moon needs to be watched at all times and birds, who are sent by Them to spy, need to be destroyed. No Aeonkin may ever befriend a bird, an Echo, and no Aeonkin may ever wear the feathers of these beings.

*A wolf with a bird familiar may not enter the pack without discussion with the pack leader first.

Do not cause harm and help those in need of it

There's nothing to be won through violence, on the contrary as the loss of control due to anger or pain may allow Them to slip into a wolf's mind. Conflicts are to be solved with words, preferably, but it does not mean that Allruh is unable to defend itself. Strangers in need of help, however, need to be assisted as this misfortune may be caused by Them. Such beings are then to be assessed in order to figure out whether they can be cured or not.

Do not dream

Allruh wolves always hope for a dreamless sleep as stories speak of wolves that have been afflicted through these events. If a wolf dreams, they need to speak to their Apothecaries or Scholars in order to figure out the meaning of this dream.

The Lightbreaker Procedure

The pack's Radiance, chosen by fate, is seen as the light that keeps Them away and only leaves their position through death. In very rare circumstances a wolf may challenge the Radiance for the position of alpha if they have gained the support of at least three members of the Smoke Council. The Radiance may only be challenged with good reason, for example, if they are displaying clear signs of affliction, as leaving this position empty for too long endangers the whole pack.

The Lightbreaker Procedure is a difficult test for the Radiance and their challenger in which both wolves need to prove that they are able to be the light the pack needs. An assessment, a fight and of course the whole pack of Allruh passing judgement on the contestants.

This event always takes place in a moonless night, so that They may not interfere.

If the Radiance fails the Procedure, they may be chased out of the pack if they prove to be too dangerous for Allruh.

Faculties (Tasks)

Guardian(s)

The wolves that keep Allruh safe. They are trained guards and fighters, responsible for border security as well as leading battles against Them and whoever They have been able to control. Guardians are often seen patrolling the territory or training to keep their muscles strong and senses sharp. If big prey needs to be hunted, Keepers usually turn to the strong Guardians to organize a hunt.

Keeper(s)

As the caretakers of the territory, Keepers are the ones expected to know everything that is going on within their home. They are responsible for knowing all the routes, the status of the prey and common herb patches as well as making sure that the tunnels of the Crypt are secure. Keepers know the best routes to take and which herds to chase and hills to pick from, they care for the prey-life to ensure a steady supply remains in the valley and make sure that their home is a home. Their work stops right at the border, where the Envoys take over.

Envoy(s)

Quick footed and clever minded networkers of the pack, who are tasked with diplomacy. Envoys need to possess high thinking capabilities and a certain sense of carefulness when dealing with outsiders. These wolves act as messengers of Allruh and also need to be able to swiftly traverse all kinds of terrain. Additionally, Envoys are tasked with cartographing new tunnels within mountain massives before giving these passages to the Keepers for housekeeping.

Apothecary(ies)

The health of Allruh is on high priority, which is why they pride themselves in excelling at training new healers. These wolves are highly capable and closely work together with the Scholars to make sure that the wolves of Allruh are healthy in body and mind. Apothecaries are often leaders in creating new effigies out of bird corpses, a macabre task that is believed to warn Them off.

Scholar(s)

Knowledge is the most precious treasure of Allruh and needs to be remembered by the Scholars. They are researchers as well as teachers, retelling the pack's history and remembering the deceased by cataloguing the stars. An important duty is sharing their knowledge with the pack's Starkin, watching over the young ones, teaching them all they need to know and helping their parents to grow them into fine Aeonkin. The Collection Hall is being handled by the Scholars.
