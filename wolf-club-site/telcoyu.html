"In the swamps to the north of the domain, the Mother's children lay in the shadows."

Shrouded in darkness, the wolves of Telcoyu live tucked away in their swamp home. Eccentric and eerie beings not unlike the shadows whom they worship, these wolves bravely welcome the darkness as part of their beings, and in the dreaded holy grounds they deem as their home they prosper as a unit — as a brotherhood. Vektren was not an unfamiliar name to be whispered across the lands: an ancient cult, or guild, whose reputation was built upon blood and fear, and a great deal of scorn. Their sinister offerings echoed throughout the lands, the assets and core trade of their existence: the hearts of living beings, keys to safeguarding the Underworld. Yet soon those whispers turned into shouts and questions. Their existence of silent shadows had been illuminated.

Seeking secrecy once more, the pack split. Their former patron moved south to other sects. The rest moved north with a new Soigner leading them through the domain and into the shadows once more.

Intrepid and cunning individuals, these wolves seek no glory nor lust for mortal power but aim for spiritual ascension and heed the call of their unholy masters: This wicked family does not welcome those weak of mind and body, but the ones who are ready to challenge mortal rules for the sake of divine service. United in their dark deeds, these misfits look after each other and have a deep understanding of the delicate metaphysical structure they believe to belong to, with respect, loyalty, religion and devotion as their bedrock. Above all, they are a hardworking, dutiful and close-knit bunch who do not tolerate lethargy and work together as each wolf pulls their own weight for the sake of the whole and use the adversities of their lifestyle to harden themselves physically and emotionally to face the world.

Telcoyu is everything but dull, however. Within their sacred abode lies love, passion, tenacity and equality. They enjoy the pleasures of life with the same fire as any other, and don't see their task to be different than any other. Together they pray, they fight, they prosper, and they die — only to be united again in the afterlife.

History

"We left our trace south of the domain."

The story of our origin is like many others of our sect. Vague and unreliable, time has twisted it in ways we cannot know, but it is ours all the same.

As the world grew and changed, we split from the roots we had once been embedded in. We, four-legged creatures of fur and fang, traveled under the guidance of our Mother and Father for most of our origin. From the earth beneath our paws. The warmth of our bodies brought from their approval as blood from the hearts of others pumped and flowed like the streams that cut through the lands. They created us; with that knowledge, our predecessors took care of them in return.

We wolves had our fill of prey of the land and sea, yet we did our best to keep a balance. Everything we took was cared for and cultivated.

As the seasons passed, the pack grew, and soon, the land of our origins faded. Our call never did; instead, it became a part of us.

The wolves of the Tél originally lived in a land not so far from what is known. They lived peacefully and opened their pack to all wolves willing to join the brotherhood. After all, they just wanted to give back to their Mother and Father. Sadly, their naivety was a mistake that ended up costing them everything.

They'd grown soft. Lost their way, seeing the Mother and Father as mere figures to fawn over. They'd withered their roots until only a rooting stump remained of a once proud sect.

When a pack settled in less than a day's trip from Tél's territory, there was little thought of why. Instead, the Soigner sent wolves to this new pack with gifts of welcome and an invitation to their next festivities. Their new Voisin were a strange pack, strict and zealous, almost like them, a regal pack that always chose to use almost serpent-like words.

The Télian were called half-savage creatures. They lived in swamps and survived by cultivating the nothingness around them. Their wants were few, and their habits were simple and easily manipulated.

They tore our woods to shreds, they tore into our culture with every snarling word, and in the end, tore all of Tél apart.

Wolves were going missing, leaving the lands like their ancestors weren't a part of its soil. In a way, some older wolves envied them, and the younger wolves couldn't help their wandering thoughts of revenge. Tél would never be the perfect creature the Voisin saw themselves as. And in that, Tél was not at fault, yet it didn't stop the pain that came from it.

That is until she came.

A raven cut through the silent, solemn night one autumn day. With it, a she-wolf, heavy with pups yet determined, walked straight into the middle of Télian territory. She spurred on the masses with a single call. Telling of packs like theirs, a sect of the original. Wayward wolves who had lost sight of the Mother and the Father.

In the night, she came, ruling the pack's attention with visions of the underworld and the gods they'd all but forgotten. In the morning, she led them away from the territory of old. The fires of the forest engulfed the Voisin, lighting their way.

Their neighboring pack didn't break them; they did change them, though. Tél was no longer soft. Instead, they became ruthless, finally embracing their origins with the help of a lone she-wolf.

In Le Grand Dérangement, the pack cut ties with their homeland, traveling in search of a place to belong. In the end, signs from their gods led them to a home in the old oaks. Here, they settled and grew. Here, the she-wolf became their Soigner. Here, the pups she carried were born. They grew together. Taking knowledge from their land and with help from their gods, they grew. Becoming a sect the truest Vektreni would be proud of. Seasons passed, and with it, its new blood grew. All eager to help shape the pack and maybe even someday a sect of their own.

Until one day her daughter walked in her paw prints...

Vektren had lived in the domain for generations. The pack that always lived in the shadows had slowly come to light as war with packs and creatures of the hive wreaked havoc upon the domain. No more could they survive as they had been. Patron Nimh and her mates have left the old holy lands, along with other Vektreni, to find secrecy with other sects to the south. Yet still some wolves remain. Trying to keep their claim to the domain. This time with a new Soigner by their sides.

Foreign Relations

"When bitten by a snake, one does not trust that they will not be bitten by another. Though not all snakes are bad, caution should always be used."

War can bring wolves together, but in the case of Telcoyu this is the last thing they desire. Their secrecy was lost after they joined in the battle, a detriment to their goals. The pack is fueled by their relations with the outside world, despite their guarded and introverted society. But caution is needed in these times. Telcoyu tends to represent their pack with excelling business and mean no harm to strangers unless deemed necessary. The individual wolf is treated with interest as any wolf is seen as a potential hirer and is treated as such - unless they are the designated target. Yet, packs are studied closely and watched for weakness. Some see Vektren's livelihood as a petty game of death, to simply defy the moral rules of wolfkind out of spite, while others see it as a spiritual bond of servitude to their dark Gods.

This politeness is displayed only outside territory, however. They consider their lands sacred, and foreigners are never allowed to set foot on such holy grounds. The outskirts are heavily guarded with traps, surrounded by treacherous rivers and hostile vegetation, and protected by Telcoyu's fearsome guardians; the Ours. Trespassing outsiders are chased and killed on sight unless the situation is exceptional in some way.

Our diplomats, the Hibou, have not spoken yet with our neighbors since the war, but with the world settling down around them, it's time to meet their Voisin. Afterall, Telcoyu views all information on their neighbors as vital. Learning about and from their wolf brethren in different places that are all connected by the lands and waterways that flow through the domain. It's important so that the pack can remain secret yet informed.

Culture

"We wolves come from many before us. We change and grow like the trees surrounding us, but in the end we are the same."

Telcoyu has always been a pack in need of connections. Though not in the way one might think. Connections have caused them devastation in the past, this trauma has made them cautious and more thoughtful about the world. The Tél had been living open for so long that in a way the pack had forgotten their ways. Telcoyu has learned from the mistakes of their previous packs in this way. All connections needed can be found in the ground under paw, the brotherhood, and those that choose to join their ranks.

The new Soigner and those of the past have always helped to shape what has been bent and broken. Growth comes from change and adversity. Together with the brotherhood they will form this new way of life.

The pack understands the importance of the world physically and spiritually. Nature is a life force of its own and is to be respected. The trees are a part of the pack and family like your own blood would be. In this same way, the Mother and Father teach them how to thrive in their world so that one day they may join them as well.

Religion

"Our mother and father covered the lands in darkness and fire. Shielding us as they rained terror over our enemies."

Telcoyu has two deities as primal Gods, and believes that the souls of fallen brothers and sisters roam in this world as protectors of those who still serve, while not at The Father's side in the Great War.

They keep their Gods pleased by performing Offering Rituals where the hearts of eliminated targets, be it wolf or prey, are buried into the sacred grounds of the Reliquary, binding its owner's soul to the underworld's protectors. This peculiarity of Telcoyu is often shown to outsiders during encounters, the importance of the heart being often expressed, the reason why Telcoyu is usually referred to as "Cult" instead of "Pack".

The Shunned Ones

Just like a pup with their parents, Telcoyu's gods gave them life. And in the end they will end up back with those who created them. From the beginning the Mother and the Father have watched over their children. They traveled with every sect as the cult split. Tél's gods only ever gave them the truth. Leading them through the Soigner to a better life and bringing those lost back into the fold.

The Mother — The Goddess of Darkness

A figure of wisdom and maternity, the Mother is the spouse of Father, the god of death. Depicted as a she-wolf with fur like the night sky and eyes like the moon, with figures of feline and serpent alike, she is a figure veiled in serenity and silence, grace and mystery. One of the First, the Mother cared for everything wrong. Calling the Void home and keeping with her the souls of all those in the shadows.

The Father — The God of Death

Previously mortal, the Father earned his godhood through proving his worth. A general of the great army, and mate of the Mother. Depicted as a he-wolf with fur as dark as the underworld, eyes like fire, the horns of a bison, the Father is an imposing figure. The sight of him brings thoughts of war, violence, death, and the underworld raging forward. Every sacrificed heart shall become part of his army and with it the great army grows ever stronger.

Other Recognized Figures

These figures are not worshiped and instead figures of other beliefs used in the teaching of their religion. Villains and champions; fools and tricksters. They all hold no more value than a fairytale in comparison to the Mother and Father.

Chlomóon — the pale charlatan

Their Chlomóon was said to be the creator. An all knowing, all seeing being that had chosen them as their chosen few. The Tél was but a speck of mud covering their pale coat. But in fact it was the opposite. Their "god" was a charlatan. A liar and a villain to all those opposed to them. A false prophet that in the end led to their enemies doom.

Tahyo — big hungry dog (black wolf)

Tahyo is our champion and trickster all in one. After all, everything good has a side of darkness. Commonly depicted as a towering black wolf, Tahyo is the Father's champion. A part of his Great Army, he helps the brotherhood in times of need and tricks outsiders as well as Bouki.

Ciel — sky & heaven (white fawn)

A child, a fawn, a symbol of innocence and naivete. A specter of the prey that have been hunted down. A poor creature depicted as an all white fawn, barely a few weeks old. Ciel is usually used as a figure in pup's tales, showing the pups a figure like them to describe the dangers of the world.

Bouki — the victim of Tahyo's trickery (cross fox)

The dimwitted fool and victim of our tricks and games. Bouki is a small spirit, depicted as a cross colored fox. The fox is usually a trickster in nature, but in these stories most of Bouki's are turned back on them.

Beliefs and Atheism

Diversity in religion is welcomed within the pack, as long as you believe in the Mother and Father as the superior gods. All wolves of Telcoyu are expected to accept that others have different beliefs and opinions. Those found criticizing the Mother and Father are to be reeducated. Any wolves found to be forcing their outsider religion onto others or discriminating against others for their beliefs are to be punished.

Atheism is also permitted, though wolves that deny beliefs are still bound to all it implies - meaning the non-believers are still obliged to make decisions taking the cult and its tenets into consideration, and will be pinned as traitors if neglect follows the way of the faithful.

Rituals and Ceremonies

Telcoyu holds several rituals and ceremonies for almost every event across the day. These are led by the Cigale, performed at sanctuaries placed at specific points of their territory. The most important ritual is the Offering, where hearts of eliminated targets are prepared and offered to The Father, binding the souls of those killed with the God.

Other two core rituals of Telcoyians are the Initiation and the Burial, the begin and end of the life of a Telcoyian respectively.

Pack Life

"We seek your weary and unwanted, your crude and rough wolves. Those who've yet to find home in the wilds of the world. Bring them to us so that they may join our pattes."

Telcoyu is a community of close-knit wolves.

Life is centered around helping both the land and one another thrive. The world is cruel and dangerous, here the wilds are not tamed, but a part of the wolves who call Telcoyu their home need this. Doing your part is incredibly important because of this. Wolves police each other with respect. Tasks are chosen with passion so that they're completed with the highest degree of care. Those who choose to join this pack will soon come to realize that those you call pack mates are more than just that. They are your kin and they will look after you as such. The freedom of choice and the happiness of being secure in it are incredibly important to the pack. These things can only be gained through working together.

In the pack, wolves are called by rank as well as a few other terms. Mes pattes, translated to mean my paws, is the typical term used as we all walk in the paws of the other. Brother and sister are also used.

The pack itself tries its very best to take care of the environment around it. They block up the holes of living trees in the hopes that creatures who find trees their home will find use in trees that have passed. Healthy trees are marked with circles of fallen branches and twigs around the base. Circles symbolize a life cycle and are incredibly important symbols to the pack.

Laws

"To break our laws is to break the trust of the brotherhood as a whole."

Tenets of the Spirit

  • Never dishonor the Gods. To do so is to invoke the Wrath of The Mother, cursing the brotherhood and marking the day of the Purge.
  • Never betray Telcoyu or its secrets. To do so is to dishonor the Gods. Common/known betrayals: giving information away, freeing trapped intruders or serfs, leaving the pack.
  • Never desecrate the hallowed altars or the sanctified pool. To do so is to dishonor the Gods.
  • Never give an offering any other end but the rightful ritual. To do so is to dishonor the Gods.
  • Never disregard, assail, steal or kill a dark brother or dark sister. To do so is to dishonor the Gods.
  • Dishonoring the Gods marks one as a renegade: Banishment from Telcoyu's ranks and punishment by death.

Tenets of the Flesh

  • Do not openly declare allegiance with Telcoyu to strangers if not in business. Anonymity is crucial to the pack's safety.
  • The cult and its safety comes above one's life. It's an honor dying protecting Telcoyu's secrets.
  • It's forbidden to disobey or refuse to carry out an order from a superior.
  • Do not pretend possessing divine power granted only to a Herald without the use of herbs or the like. Punishment for fraud will follow.
  • Respect your task's boundaries regarding leaving permissions.
  • Unnecessary quarrels are not tolerated. Ours must carry out punishment for the involved if physical level is reached.
  • Eat in order of rank. Pregnant females, lactating mothers and pups are allowed to feast first with their Patron(s).
  • Juveniles and pups are not to be mistreated, but taken into the cult for reeducation. Sacrificing, harming or physically punishing whelps is forbidden.
  • Care for the land as your care for your brotherhood. Game is scarce, do not overhunt. The flora is fragile, do not abuse it.
  • It is forbidden to take a mate outside of Telcoyu's ranks.
  • Lovers can be chosen freely, as there are no pairing restrictions such as amount of partners or gender. Those wishing to make their bond official however must wed before the eyes of the Gods.
  • Adultery, however frowned upon, is not seen as law breaking, but must be resolved peacefully. The matrimonial bond dissolutes and the divorced pair loses the perks given to spouses.
  • She-wolves should not bear pups before seeking permission so as to prevent undue strain on the pack's thin resources.
  • At the need of a litter to strengthen the pack, both parents must be fully involved on the whelps raising, regardless of emotional connection. Wedding is not required, but permission is.
  • Disrespecting or harming a hirer while carrying Telcoyu's name is forbidden.
  • Any outsider that is deemed to have potential of becoming a good asset to the Great War may be killed.

Laws apply equally to all wolves of Telcoyu. Though circumstances play a part in determining the sentencing for each wolf, Telcoyu has learned to understand that "ruthlessness is mercy upon ourselves." Punishments are not given without a trial and serious understanding of the sentencing.

Punishments include: Labor. Re-education. Serfdom. Cicatrice (scarification). Banishment. Death.

Tasks

"The pack is like a wolf. It has teeth and claws that protect, a mind to keep sharp, a stomach to keep full, and a soul to keep soothed."

Work should not be the cause of heart ache. The pack's youth is taught this from a young age. Growing with the knowledge that a task is not taken to burden a wolf with work, but to ease the weight from each pack mate. Joining Telcoyu comes with the understanding that within the year, a task will be taken. Afterall, to be a part of the pack is to help the pack.

Wolves taking on a task promise "faire serment" to help their pack. To take an oath or to swear, not only to the pack, but to themselves as well. All wolves will take a serment for their task and with it will join the ranks of their pack.

Ours — Protector

The world beyond the marshes is harsh and cruel to those who are weak. Mind, body, and soul must be balanced to face the cruelty that comes from the wilds. Wolves who desire to defend their home from those who wish to take it find their place with the Ours.

Fouine — Healer

The wolf is a complicated creature with many intricacies and nuances. The wolves that become their keepers find joy and challenge in their renewal. The fouines are healers, witch doctors, medics, and herbalists in every sense. No one way of healing is treated higher than another. These methods are combined for the best care for their pack. With their specialties, they also work in alchemy. Creating poisons to protect their kin and learning how the physical world can be used to the pack's advantage.

Cigale — Cleric

Our religion is true. This is a humble way of life. We live and we die for the Mother and Father, and the circle continues as we give life to those who will come when we say goodbye. Wolves are wayward creatures and at times guidance is needed. As well as performing all rituals, Cigales give this guidance in their stories and songs of creatures and spirits unknown to this world.

Tchoc — Hunter

The wolves of Telcoyu care deeply for their new home and the lands they have settled on. They also know they are not the only beings a part of it. Predators and prey play their own part in the world's cycle. Though the wolves of Telcoyu need to hunt to survive, that does not mean it's done without thought. Death is a part of life and as they take on contracts along the way to aid the Mother and Father. Violence isn't without consequence or abundance after all.

Lapin — Scout

The world is full of wonders and at times staying put in the territory can be hard for some of Telcoyu's wolves. Adventure is part of a wolf's spirit and this task encourages it wholeheartedly. These wolves act as scouts, traders, species, and trappers; working alongside the other tasks to help the pack as a whole. In a way, they are like the rivers that flow, bringing home its newly collected resources and secrets from the world around them.

Hibou — Recorder

After recent history, it's been more important than ever to understand their past and work towards their future. With this in mind, the Soigner created a task so that they may know of the world surrounding them as well as the world they make for themselves. Our Hibou act as not only record keepers, but also diplomats, envoys, and mappers. Shaping and writing the pack's history with each changing season.
