Character Creation: Overview

  • Only wolves and approved Other Creatures (see list) may be characters. Wolf-hybrids (i.e. wolfdogs, coywolves, etc.) are not permitted.
  • Characters cannot be fan-based. You can take inspiration from tropes, but you cannot too closely resemble specific characters from movies, shows, or games. We don't want to see carbon copies of Balto, Ghost, or Bolt!
  • Characters are not permitted magical or unnatural powers. They might believe they have powers, but cannot actually have powers.
    • The same goes for dreams! They may interpret their dreams as visions, but these are not actually visions - just dreams!
  • Characters created with ancestral ties to former ("disbanded") Wolf Club packs, or relatives of current or former characters from disbanded packs are not allowed unless given explicit permission only from a current member of the group with direct ties to the pack. This can either be the former Pack Leader, or a player with a character who was in or from a pack that disbanded who your character will be related to.
  • Explicitly labeled mental disorders should not be officially defined on a character's biography, nor should they be used as a reference point in relation to a character's specific behavior or action.
    • Any inspiration pulled from existing mental disorders, whether through experience, research, or coincidence, should remain nameless or otherwise be non-specific in its depiction, especially in public published material in Wolf Club. Furthermore, even in its nameless state, the writing of mental disorders or mental instability should avoid exaggerated stereotypes or caricatures.
    • It is important to consider that whenever touching on a disability of any kind that there should always be a level of respect to its portrayal, likewise it is equally important to note that not all experiences share the same track. We never know what another person goes through behind the screen, so make sure that portrayal and judgement are held to the standard of understanding, fairness, and respect.
  • Looking to adopt out a family member or pups? Check out this handy dandy template we've got right here!

First Characters

  • New members start with only one character, which must be a pack-wolf. After a season (3 months) you may bring in more characters, including Loners and Other Creatures.
  • First characters cannot have mutations. Mutations cost our in-game currency, Bones, and thus cannot be purchased when first entering the game.
  • First characters must be at minimum juvenile. Pups are not playable as first characters due to needing guardians.

Other Characters

  • After the three-month wait period between submitting your first and second characters, there is a 1-month wait period between any other characters submitted to the group.
    • The exception to this rule is litter tryouts, which can be applied for by members not within their new-member-3-month-cooldown period.
  • Slot occupancy in any given pack is on a first-come, first-serve basis (FCFS). Packs will only accept new characters if they are listed as OPEN on the group's front page. New characters can only join factions when they are officially approved and purchased. Notes/Tickets are processed in the order they are received.
  • Each member may have up to 10 active characters at any time. The first and second are free, and after that, new characters will cost in-game points called bones.
  • Characters can enter the Club with ONE minor accessory (i.e. a bundle of feathers or twigs). The cost of the accessory will add on to the character cost.
  • Characters can enter the Club with a familiar. The cost of the familiar will be added onto the character cost.
  • Members may not have more than three characters in a single pack at any given time. Alphas reserve the right to limit it further based on demand for their respective packs - if you have questions, please feel free to reach out to the respective alpha.
  • Members who have characters forcibly retired for inactivity during seasonal activity checks will be barred from bringing back or creating new characters for one month. This will be tracked on the staff's bone tracker. Should you have any questions about when this cooldown expires, you are welcome to reach out to staff via note to the group.
  • If you wish to change the design of an active character, you MUST Note the Group or submit a Mod Ticket to have the new design approved before implementing the change.
  • For Bones costs and restrictions on new characters, rank ups, mates, litters, accessories, faction changes, and tasks - see the Bones page.
  • Players' Loner and Other character's count may not exceed their Pack character count. Members may have up to a maximum of 3 TOTAL Loners or Other Characters at any time. So if you have…
    1 Character= No Loner or Other Creatures
    2 Characters= 1 Loner or Other Creature
    3 Characters= 1 Loner or Other Creature
    4 Characters= 2 Loners or Other Creatures
    5 Characters= 2 Loners or Other Creatures
    6-10 Characters= 3 Loners or Other Creatures
    Pups do not count towards the total until they reach Young Adult. If your loner and pack-wolf characters are equal and a pack-wolf is retired, members may not bring in another loner as their next character.

Other Creatures

Though Wolf Club is primarily a wolf group, we have a number of other playable species!

Approved Creatures

  • Coyotes
  • Bears (Black, Brown, and Grizzly)
  • Predatory Cats (Bobcats, Lynxes, and Mountain Lions)
  • Foxes (Red and Gray)
  • Canadian/US Mustelids (wolverines, badgers, otters, skunks, others)
  • Other Critters (Virginia Opossums, porcupines, raccoons)
  • Birds of Prey (falcons, owls, eagles, etc.)

Members may ask about various species (if they are not included here) through ticket or note! Species should follow these guidelines:

  • They CAN be from the coniferous or deciduous biomes of the U.S. and Canada and Northern Mexico, where wolves are found.
  • They CAN be either a predator or tree-dwelling animal which can stay out of the reach of a hunting wolf.
  • They CANNOT be a herbivorous grazer (deer, buffalo, moose, things like this).
  • They CAN be a coyote but CANNOT crossbreed with wolves.
  • Other Creatures must still conform to character design guidelines but remain within the size parameters of their species. (i.e. color, height, and weight must still be realistic to their species, not wolves)
  • Other Creatures may not join a pack, although their personal homeland/territory may overlap with pack territory.
  • Other Creatures may have familiars but the familiar must be at least 25% smaller.

Application Art Requirements

  • App art may be your own art, commissioned art, or collaborated artwork! Commissioned art and collaborated art must be credited somewhere in the deviation description.
  • Your character's reference art should primarily reside inside the main box, though minor overlaps are not a concern as long as all text and palettes are visible!
  • The main box's colors should NOT be adjusted, have textures added, or feature a background.
    • Text blurbs, scar highlights, gradient details, etc. are permitted in the main box and extras box.
    • The extras box can also feature a background, but the main box cannot.
  • Dark outlines or drop shadows are permitted in order to allow a character to pop from their background, particularly when their coats blend with the app boxes. Drop shadows or outlines can also be used on the palette below.
  • You are NOT required to use the extras box or put your character's medallion on the app - it is your choice.
  • In the possibility you leave Wolf Club or retire your character any new design rules that are added into the group in your absence will be expected to be adjusted onto your character before re-entry into the Club.

Biographical Information

Once your character is on their reference sheet and ready to be uploaded, please put the following in the description of your deviation. This form can be added to but must retain all the basic information included here! See below for what can be left off of dA (as long as it is provided somewhere!)

  • Name: (What is your character's name?)
  • Age: (Please use the simplified form of their age [pup, juvenile, etc] at minimum; including the number is optional! Pup = 0-3 months, Juvenile = 3-9 months, Young Adult = 9 months to 2-3 years, Adult 4-8 years, Aged Adult 9-11 years, or Elder = 12+ years)
  • Gender: (Biological sex and identity, if distinct - pronouns can be added too!)
  • Height: (See character creation section below; pups and juveniles may be below standard heights but should grow into an appropriate bracket)
  • Weight: (See character design rules below; pups and juveniles may be below standard weights but should grow into an appropriate bracket)
  • Build: (Describe the general body type of your character. No need to go into fur pattern, we can see that from the picture!)
  • Territory: (If they are in a pack, which pack? Loners/creatures do not require an official territory but their favourite area may be listed)
  • Family: (Do they have any family in Wolf Club? If not, you may include NPC family who are not located in the Club, if marked as NPCs!)
  • Rank: (If in a pack, what rank are they? If they're a loner, leave blank) *Note: all new members start out as Omega's without tasks
  • Personality: (200 word minimum)
  • Pre-Group History: (300 word minimum, unless the character was born to group characters in which case Pre-Group should be a brief explanation of the parents and pup's birth)
  • Group History: (This comes through RP! Keep a log of your interactions, drawings, bones, etc here)
  • Other: (Anything else you'd like to add)

Important

Biographies on dA must include the following:

  • Name
  • Gender/Pronouns
  • Size
  • Pack
  • Family*
  • Rank and Task
  • Personality
  • Pre-Group History
  • Anything else can be linked offsite (google docs, toyhouse) as long as the full biography information is provided somewhere!
  • "Group History" and "Other" are the ONLY sections that can be replaced with a link!
  • *"Family" can be left off if your character has no immediate family (parents, siblings, pups if any) played by other players.

Biographical Information (Details)

Names

  • Names may not be held or reserved. If two people want the same name, it is entirely dependent on who submits their character and payment first! Exception: if players pay for a pup in a litter, they can reserve the name when they pay.
  • Wolves can have the same name as another character no longer in the group.
  • Wolves can have the same name as active characters if they go by an entirely different name or nickname primarily (for instance, if two wolves are named Charlotte but one goes exclusively by Charlie).

Age

In real life, wolves are pups until roughly 6 months old, and juveniles until roughly a year. In order to keep the Club flowing, we have halved this time to reach young adulthood. Ages are distributed as such:

Pup= 0-3 months
Juvenile= 3-9 months
Young Adult= 9 months to 2-3 years
Adult= 4-8 years
Aged Adult= 9-11 years
Elder= 12+ years
  • Pups should age up to juvenile approximately 3 real-life months after birth, and similarly, juveniles should age up to young adult after approximately six real life months. After a wolf reaches young adulthood, you may age them up at whatever speed you deem appropriate.
  • If bringing in a pup character who is NOT from a Wolf Club litter adoption, you MUST have a Guardian. Please consult the "Extra Info on Pups" section in the Wolf Club Mates and Pups Guide for requirements on Pups and Guardians.

Gender

  • Wolf Club is open and welcoming to all expressions of gender identity. Please inform us of whatever pronouns your wolf may use, the gender they identify as, and other relevant information.
  • You must list the biological sex of your character somewhere on their app sheet.

Height and Weight

  • Males: 32"-36" (though unusually small males may be only 30")
  • Females: 30"-34" (though unusually large females may be 36")
  • Characters cannot exceed 36" unless they have gigantism. They may be as small as 28" if considered undersized, and may be ever so slightly shorter if they have dwarfism. Any mutations must be purchased.
  • Weight range should fall between 60 lbs and 120 lbs. Based on size, a wolf may be as little as 50 lbs and as heavy as 130 lbs. We understand that overweight and underweight wolves do exist, but a reason must be run by the admin team before making a character fall outside of these limitations.

Ratio:

30''= 60 lbs (50-70 lbs range)
31''= 70 lbs (60-80 lbs range)
32''= 80 lbs (70-90 lbs range)
33''= 90 lbs (80-100 lbs range)
34''= 100 lbs (90-110 lbs range)
35''= 110 lbs (100-120 lbs range)
36''= 120 lbs (110-130 lbs range)

Females will generally hit the average to lower range, while males will more likely hit the average to higher range. Anything above or below the basic range would be considered overweight or malnourished, respectively.

Build

Describe what your character's build is like. Terms such as lean, athletic, broad, stocky, short, tall, angular, etc. can be used, or more descriptive paragraphs about areas that are more muscular or toned - you can also just describe a character's build as average!

Family

  • Any characters who are part of Wolf Club should be listed here. Linking their application sheets is optional.
  • Wolves cannot enter the Club with a mate, unless the mate was an NPC from before their time in the Club and is dead or separated.
  • NPC family members (characters who are not in the Club, and are not played by people) are not permitted in the group. They are either alive beyond the Club, or dead, and cannot be referenced as alive within a standing Club pack.
  • NPC families can be listed on your application sheet as long as they are marked as NPCs!
  • Should potential, personal plotlines involve NPC family, you MUST Note the Group or submit a Mod Ticket to approve such a plot! Ultimately, though, we want you to plot with your fellow members. The same goes for family adoptions - like any other adoption, they must be approved by the admin team!

Personality

  • Personality should be well-rounded, including both positive and negative traits - even in villainous or angelic characters! Nobody is perfect, and having a bit of imperfection makes characters more relatable and believable!
  • We do ask that characters are more than just one main trait! Nobody is defined solely by a single personality trait, and we have seen a lot of characters in the past whose only defining trait is flirtatiousness, sex drive, aggression, etc. It is acceptable to have traits like this, but a character needs to be more than this to be accepted!

Pre-Group History

  • Character backstories should explain where they came from. What was their early life like? Their family? A potential pack they grew up in? What drew them to the Club? How did they end up in the pack you have chosen?
  • Please note that characters CANNOT come from in-game packs without discussing with the administrative team! It's best to come from outside of the Club!
  • Character backstories featuring triggering or mature content will be carefully and critically evaluated on a case-by-case basis by the staff. Some characters with these backstories may not be permitted in the group for the sake of community safety and welfare.
  • Backstories of this nature, if approved, must possess proper trigger warnings.
  • Backstories CANNOT feature humans! In Wolf Club, there are no humans.
  • On your application sheet, you may want to keep a running list of art and RPs your character has been involved in, or a link to a separate document! This does not have to be filled when your character enters the group.

Other

  • Trivia and other information, such as likes and dislikes, can be included here!

Design Guide

Fur Color

  • Color. Coat colors may range from almost pure white through various shades of blond, cream, tan, reddish and ochre to grays, silvers, browns and blacks.
  • Colors should not appear too saturated, and you may be asked to desaturate characters upon review if they are too bright or unnaturally colored. Color combinations should not be too unrealistic or high contrast, and will be reviewed upon a case-by-case basis.

Markings

This is a non-exhaustive list and cannot account for every possibility, however we have tried to make a basic guideline of the most common markings we typically don't allow. All designs entering Wolf Club are reviewed on a case-by-case basis, as we do not want to limit the creativity of our members so much as assure a semi-realistic appearance to the characters within the Club. If you are unsure about a marking it is always acceptable to send a Note to the Group or submit a Mod Ticket to ask for a review, we're happy to help!

The admin team reserves the right to ask for alterations to be made, should it not fit the Wolf Club guidelines.

Ticking

Ticking is small pieces or ticks of color in a wolf's pelt, it is typically a way to stylistically illustrate more complicated coat textures in a simplified way. There are two types of ticking. The first method will frequently occur around and trail away from larger markings along the shoulders, back and tail. This kind of ticking can be high contrast in comparison to the base coat, but should logically gather around the markings they "connect" to. Please see the "Flecking" section below for the second method.

  • Ticking is permitted but should not look like spots and should be uniform. It usually occurs in the shoulders and back.
  • Should be small, clumpy ticking of any color that gathers around or logically connects to a larger marking of that color.

Flecking

The second type of ticking is to create subtle ticks throughout the pelt to indicate a variation in coat color. With this kind of ticking, we like to see it being fairly low contrast and sporadic so that it looks like it belongs to the same coat color, and does not look like distinct spots or hard markings.

  • Flecking may appear darker or lighter than the color it overlaps but must not be too high in contrast (ex. White on Black)
  • Size and shape may vary but flecking must not look like small blobs or spots
  • Flecking may be present anywhere on the body or over the full body
  • Freckle/mole-like markings on the cheeks, nose bridge and muzzle are acceptable.

Border Markings

Border Markings are a thin, usually darker "stripe" of colors or shapes that border larger marking shapes. It is more of a transition color or a bordering shape rather than a distinctive marking. Instances of this marking that are not approved are generally when it becomes a thin string of color "outlining" larger marking shapes, when layers of colors get too thin/compacted, or if it becomes too much. This marking type should be an accent, not a major feature!

  • Border markings may appear lighter or darker that the colors on either side of it
  • Border markings must be large enough to not be confused as a "stripe." Thin strips of color around larger markings that look too much like outlines will not be accepted.

Sock Markings

Some leg markings are intentionally or unintentionally designed to reference glove or wristband-like shapes. This is frequently featured on sparkledog-type designs, but they are generally discouraged here. A general rule of thumb is that if it's a sparkledog marking, looks too much like a fingerless glove, or is not a plausible wolf marking, it will likely not be accepted.

  • Leg markings must be symmetrical and should not appear uneven or "splotchy."
  • No high contrast "toe socks," "wristbands," or "gloves." The exception being high contrast toes on a wolf's base color with no socks present.
  • Markings may be drawn hard lined or blurred but should conform to basic contours of the legs.
  • Gradient leg markings are allowed as long as they are not high contrast.
  • No domestic looking white or light colored "socks" that are not plausible in wolves.

Implausible Markings

Sometimes there are markings that don't pertain to our more specific guidelines that we reserve the right to approve or not. We do allow small "quirk" markings on a case-by-case basis, but generally if a marking stands out too much or is inherently non-wolf, it is unlikely to be accepted. If in doubt - look up some real wolf images to reference!

  • No domestic dog-like markings. This includes large asymmetrical markings, spots and patches (not always necessarily white). No piebalds, rosettes, or merle coloration.
  • Intricate, unnatural markings are not permitted. This includes stripes, swirls, symbols, and zig-zag markings.
  • Most if not all markings should be symmetrical. Small asymmetrical markings are allowed, but must be kept to a minimum and will be looked over on a case-by-case basis.

Minimal White | High Contrast

  • Pure white markings should be kept to a minimum unless they rest on a pale design or your character is albino. Adding too much pure white markings runs the risk of a design looking too unnatural.
  • Try to avoid extreme black with stark white or other unrealistic contrasts. This can pertain to ticking / flecking markings as well.

Fur Styles

  • No large hairdos. Small hair tufts are allowed but nothing... extreme!
    • Hairdos that cover the eyes will be asked to trim down.
  • Moderate feathering on the legs is allowed, but shouldn't drag the ground.

Eyes

  • Irises can be variations of yellow, gold, amber, brown, green, hazel, blue, or gray.
  • Bi-colored eyes (heterochromia) are permitted!
  • Sclera should not be an outlandish hue, but can be a lighter version of the iris, as well as black or pale yellow (or just white).
  • Pupils should be black or very dark, unless the character is blind or has an eye injury.

Other Features

  • Noses and paw-pads must be darker than the wolf's base coat, unless they are albino. Lighter-colored paw pads are permitted on light colored wolves but should never be lighter than the wolf's fur. Pink paw pads and pink noses are allowed for non-albino characters but must not be high in contrast to the wolf's coat.
  • No stark white or unnatural colored noses or pawpads.
  • No unnatural patterns, stripes or shapes. Spotted or pigmented noses should be kept to a minimum with no heavy contrasting colors.
  • Mutations such as albinism, hyperdontia, etc. are permitted as long as evidence of it exists in real-world wolves.

Scarring

Characters cannot enter the Club with an injury too unrealistic to survive in the wild. If you are uncertain as to whether the injury you want to give your character is allowed, please Note the Group or submit a Mod Ticket! Injuries received in RP that are severe should also be approved by admins prior to altering characters. The following are not permitted.

  • Severe head or jaw injuries
  • Multiple missing or broken limbs
  • Severe burns
  • Items embedded in flesh
  • Exposed skeleton

A member may dispute any of these rules but they must use a real wolf photo and/or research in order to back up their argument. The admins will deliberate these on a case-by-case basis.
