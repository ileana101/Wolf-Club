## Right after they leave (2 minutes)

- What surprised you:  
  - It took nearly 48 minutes of independent searching to find a piece of information that does exist on the site, she'd been searching using synonyms rather than the exact term the page used, and was embarrassed once she realized it had been findable the whole time.   
- One thing you must not forget:  
  - The mismatch between DeviantArt's wording and the site's wording ("characters cannot be fan-based") is likely a real source of confusion, not just a navigation problem.   
- Did you explain or rescue anywhere?  
  - Yes, but only after independent search had already run its course. Misty searched on her own for the full \~48 minutes before any help was given; the rescue (side-by-side screenshots of the admin site and DeviantArt) came afterward, once it was clear she wouldn't find it unaided. *The 48-minute unaided search stands as valid data; only what happened after that point is "with help."*
